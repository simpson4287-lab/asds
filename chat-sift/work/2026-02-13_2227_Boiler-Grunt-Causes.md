## Most Common Causes (UK Gas Boiler Context)

### 1. Delayed Ignition (“Mini Gas Explosion” Effect)
If gas builds slightly before ignition, you get a **deep thud / grunt when it lights**.

**Symptoms**
- Single loud thump at startup  
- Happens right at flame ignition  
- Sometimes worse after sitting idle  

**Cause**
- Dirty burner
- Worn ignition electrodes
- Incorrect gas/air mix
- Low gas pressure

⚠️ This is a **Gas Safe engineer job**, not DIY.

---

### 2. Expansion / Contraction of Pipework
If it’s more of a metallic groan or bang:

**Symptoms**
- Happens as system heats
- Can sound like it’s inside walls
- Worse in cold weather

This is pipe expansion against joists or clips.

Less serious — but noisy.

---

### 3. Pump Struggling on Startup
If the grunt sounds like a **mechanical groan or heavy vibration** for a few seconds:

**Symptoms**
- Noise lasts 2–10 seconds
- Boiler vibrates slightly
- Radiators may heat unevenly

Could be:
- Stiff circulation pump
- Air in system
- Low system pressure

Check:
- What’s the pressure reading? (Should usually be 1–1.5 bar cold)

---

### 4. Heat Exchanger Kettling (Scale Build-Up)
More common in hard-water areas.

**Sound**
- Deep rumble or growl
- Like a kettle starting to boil
- Can sound aggressive

This happens when scale traps heat and water flashes to steam locally.

Usually needs:
- Chemical flush
- Possibly heat exchanger cleaning
