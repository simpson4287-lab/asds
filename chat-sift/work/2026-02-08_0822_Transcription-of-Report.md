# National Curriculum Core Subjects

### English

Chris has made good progress in reading this year. He has become a fluent reader. He now needs to read a range of books in order to find his personal preferences. Chris has some good ideas for story writing and has written some very good poems. He has started to write better story plans and now needs to improve his punctuation. His spelling is good. His handwriting is usually correctly formed but could be neater. He now needs to think more about his general presentation.

---

### Mathematics

Chris has worked well in maths this year and has made good progress in number. He can calculate mentally and use written methods for +, − and x. He is sometimes unsure of ÷. He has had good results in Mental Arithmetic tests which have involved number, money and algebra. In P.E. lessons Chris has made sensible estimates of his performances and has measured accurately with tape, metre sticks and stop-watches. He has recorded his results.

---

### Science

Christopher has worked well in science this year. He has made careful observations of experiments including rotting food, rusting nails, heating and burning materials and cooking food. He has written good reports and recorded his results in tables. He has researched teeth and healthy diets using reference books and pamphlets. Chris has investigated electrical circuits.

### Religious Education
