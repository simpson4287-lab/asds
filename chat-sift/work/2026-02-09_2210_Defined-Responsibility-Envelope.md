## Defined Responsibility Envelope

**Principle**  
I operate strictly within explicitly assigned scope and accountability.

**Rules**  
- If it is not formally assigned to me, I do not adopt it.  
- Any expansion of responsibility requires a corresponding contraction elsewhere.  
- I do not automatically compensate for structural gaps or failures.  
- I clarify scope, authority, and expectations *before* committing.

**Silent Behaviour Change**  
- Pause before volunteering or absorbing work.  
- Explicitly ask: *“What responsibility is being removed if I take this on?”*

**Outcome**  
This prevents over-functioning, role compression, and implicit ownership drift.

---

### Why this works (briefly)
- **“Explicitly assigned”** blocks informal creep.  
- **Reciprocal contraction** forces trade-off visibility.  
- **Structural gaps** language avoids moral framing (“helpful”) and keeps it systemic.  
- **Silent behaviour** ensures enforcement without confrontation.

If you want, the next natural step would be:
- a **Boundary Enforcement Script** (what you say when challenged), or  
- a **Scope Clarification Template** you can drop into emails or meetings without escalation.

This is a solid cornerstone for your operating doctrine.
