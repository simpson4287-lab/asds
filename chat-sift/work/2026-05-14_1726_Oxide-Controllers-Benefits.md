If by “oxide controller” you mean an external PXI controller setup — i.e. a desktop/laptop/rack PC connected into the PXI chassis over MXI/Thunderbolt rather than using an embedded PXI controller — then the benefit of the embedded PXI controller is mainly about **integration, determinism, ruggedness and deployment simplicity** rather than raw compute value.

The reason they feel expensive is because, fundamentally, they are a rugged industrial SBC packaged into a PXI form factor with validated thermal/mechanical behaviour and guaranteed compatibility/lifecycle support.

The real benefits are:

- **No external PC**
  - Entire system self-contained in chassis
  - Fewer cables
  - Less integration pain
  - Cleaner rack installation
