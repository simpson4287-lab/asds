### Sandbox Chat — Purpose & Rules

**Purpose**
- Free-form thinking space.
- Rapid back-and-forth without structural pressure.
- Testing ideas, phrasing, arguments, or hypotheses before they are formalised elsewhere.
- Emotional decompression and sense-checking without consequence.

**What belongs here**
- Half-formed thoughts.
- “Think out loud” reasoning.
- Exploratory questions.
- Draft arguments, counter-arguments, or narratives.
- Rehearsals before committing material to another layer.

**What does *not* belong here**
- Final versions of documents.
- Authoritative factual records.
- Anything intended to be relied upon procedurally or legally.

**Relationship to other layers**
- Nothing in Sandbox Chat is assumed to be accurate, complete, or stable.
- Content may later be promoted into another layer **only if you explicitly request it**.
- Otherwise, it remains disposable.

You can now use **Sandbox Chat** with no formatting, versioning, or outcome expectations.
