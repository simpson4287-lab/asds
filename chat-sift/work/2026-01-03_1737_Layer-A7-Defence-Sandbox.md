## **A7 – Defence Sandbox**

**Purpose:**  
A controlled working layer used to *develop, test, refine, and stress-test* defence arguments and materials **without contaminating baseline documents** or evidential records.

**Status:**  
Non-evidential | Working | Iterative | Disposable until promoted

---

### **What belongs in A7**

This layer is intentionally flexible and exploratory.

**1. Argument Development**
- Draft defence theories (primary, secondary, fallback positions)
- Legal reasoning notes (mens rea, actus reus, statutory thresholds)
- Competing interpretations of facts or evidence
- “If–then” scenario logic (e.g. CPS position vs defence response)

**2. Statement Drafting & Variants**
- Alternative versions of written statements
- Tone testing (clinical / factual / legal)
- Short-form vs long-form variants
- Risk-flagged wording experiments

**3. Evidence Framing (Not Storage)**
- How evidence *could* be framed or challenged  
- Relevance tests (what helps, what harms, what is neutral)
- Exclusion arguments or weight-reduction reasoning  
⚠️ Raw evidence itself remains in **A2 / A3**

**4. Anticipated Challenges**
- Likely CPS arguments
- Weak-point identification
- Cross-examination risk notes
- “What would a hostile reader say?”

**5. Notes for Solicitor Discussion**
- Questions to raise
- Options to sanity-check
- Decisions requiring legal advice
- Areas where you defer to counsel

---

### **What does NOT belong in A7**

- Finalised statements → **A10 – Project Output**
- Factual incident record → **A4 – Incident Recall**
- Mental health history or reports → **A5**
- Formal legal process records → **A8 – Court Sandbox**
- Evidence originals → **A2 / A3**

---

### **Promotion Rule (Important)**

Nothing in **A7** is authoritative by default.

Content only moves out of A7 when it is:
- Deliberately selected
- Cleaned
- Context-checked
- And **promoted** to:
  - **A6** (Interview outputs),
  - **A8** (Court prep), or
  - **A10** (Final defence material)

This keeps your project psychologically lighter and legally safer.
