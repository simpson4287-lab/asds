# **B2 – Evidence & Documentation**

**Purpose**  
To act as the **single, authoritative repository** for all documentary and material evidence relevant to the case. This layer contains *only* items that exist independently of recollection or interpretation.

This is the evidential backbone of Project B.

---

## **What This Layer Contains**

Only **verifiable materials**, including but not limited to:

### **Official Records**
- Custody record(s)  
- Interview audio/video recordings  
- Interview transcripts (if supplied)  
- Police disclosure schedules  
- Charge sheets  
- Bail documentation  
- Court notices and listings  
- CPS correspondence (if received)

### **Physical / Digital Evidence**
- CCTV footage (or disclosure references to it)  
- Body-worn video references  
- Screenshots supplied by police  
- Photographs relied upon by the investigation  

### **Access & Disclosure Materials**
- Subject Access Request acknowledgements  
- Right of Access responses  
- Disclosure confirmation emails  
- Evidence availability notices  

### **Supporting Documents**
- Formal medical letters **only if relied upon or disclosed**  
- GP or clinician statements **only in document form**  
- Any document explicitly referenced by police or court

---

## **What This Layer Does *Not* Contain**

Strict exclusions:

- Personal recollections  
- Explanations, interpretations, or commentary  
- Mental health context not in documentary form  
- Draft arguments or defence notes  
- Emails you wrote *unless* they are evidentially relevant  
- Thoughts, impressions, or concerns  

If it did not exist **before you touched it**, it does not belong here.

---

## **Rules of Use (Critical)**

1. **No Interpretation**
   - This layer is documentary only.
   - Zero analysis, zero narrative.

2. **Original Form Preserved**
   - Documents are stored as received.
   - No rewriting, paraphrasing, or “cleaning up”.

3. **Traceability**
   - Each item must have:
     - Source (police, court, CPS, GP, etc.)
     - Date issued or received
     - Reference number (if present)

4. **Chronology Neutral**
   - Items are not reordered to tell a story.
   - They are logged, not narrated.

---

## **Relationship to Other Layers**

- **Feeds into**:
  - **B3 – Incident (Factual Core)**  
  - **B5 – Investigation & Interview**  
  - **B6 – Charges & Court Process**

- **Does not overlap with**:
  - Project A (Personal Log)
  - Any mental health reflection outside formal letters
  - Defence strategy or argumentation

---

## **Practical Benefit**

This layer ensures:
- No evidential drift  
- No accidental contradiction  
- Clear separation between *what exists* and *what is argued*  
- Immediate readiness for solicitor, CPS, or court review  

If challenged, this is the layer that answers:  
**“What documents actually exist?”**
