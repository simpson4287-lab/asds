## Profile vibe

**Positioning:** thoughtful, funny, practical, emotionally open, good with dogs, into projects/music/real conversations.

Not too heavy. Not “therapy profile”. More: *interesting bloke with depth, dry humour, and a life being built.*

---

## Main bio / “about me” style line

**Option 1 — balanced**
> Engineer by trade, dog dad by lifestyle, cabin-builder by questionable decision-making. I’m happiest with good music, a proper conversation, and something practical to fix, build or overthink.

**Option 2 — warmer**
> I’m a curious, hands-on person who likes music, dogs, building things, and conversations that go past small talk. Currently learning that “simple weekend project” is almost always a lie.

**Option 3 — slightly funnier**
> I design electronics, build things I later regret starting, and have two dogs who are probably better at dating than I am. Big fan of music, dry humour, and people who are kind without making a big performance of it.

My pick: **Option 3**. It has character and gives people several easy hooks.

---

## Hinge prompts

### Prompt: **Together, we could…**
> Go for a walk with the dogs, find a decent coffee, then accidentally spend three hours talking about music, life, weird childhood memories and whether that house project was actually a good idea.

This is good because it paints a date.

---

### Prompt: **The way to win me over is…**
> Be kind, curious, and able to laugh at yourself. Bonus points if you like dogs, good music, and don’t mind someone who gets weirdly passionate about making things work properly.

This is warm without sounding needy.

---

### Prompt: **I geek out on…**
> Music, electronics, old cars, DIY projects, and understanding how things actually work. I’m also very capable of turning a “quick fix” into a full investigative documentary.

This one gives personality and humour.

---

### Prompt: **A life goal of mine…**
> Build a home that feels calm, creative and full of dogs, music, decent food, and people who can be themselves.

This is more emotionally attractive and mature.

---

### Prompt: **Green flags I look for…**
> Emotional honesty, kindness to animals, good communication, and someone who can say “I was wrong” without making it a courtroom drama.

Strong. Slightly funny. Also filters well.

---

## Photo lineup

Use 6 photos if possible:

1. **Clear face photo** — daylight, smiling, no sunglasses.
2. **Full-body casual photo** — outdoors, relaxed.
3. **With the dogs** — but make sure your face is visible.
4. **Doing something practical** — cabin/building/DIY/car/electronics, not too messy.
5. **Music/personality photo** — gig, guitar, record collection, Radiohead-ish without looking obsessive.
6. **Social or candid photo** — with one or two people, not a big crowd.

Avoid too many selfies, car-only photos, blurry pub photos, or anything that looks like a work ID badge.

---

## What not to put on the profile

I’d avoid leading with heavy mental health, past relationship pain, family stuff, legal/work stress, or “I’m complicated”. You can be honest later, but the profile’s job is to create curiosity and safety, not give your whole autobiography.

Also avoid:
> “Ask me anything”

> “Just seeing what’s out there”

> “No drama”

> “I don’t know what to write here”

Those all waste space.
