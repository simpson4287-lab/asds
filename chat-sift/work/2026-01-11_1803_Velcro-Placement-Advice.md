[M2] AI (2026-01-11 18:03):
Based on what you have shown, the correct and practical choice is:

**Put the *hook* (rough) side on the work folder, and the *loop* (soft) side on the notepad.**

Rationale:

1. **Handling and abrasion**
   - The folder is handled, slid into bags, and rests against clothing and other documents.
   - Hook Velcro on the *folder* is less of a problem because it is usually covered by the notepad.
   - Loop Velcro on the notepad avoids snagging sleeves, paperwork, or lining when the pad is removed.

2. **Durability**
   - The hook side tolerates repeated compression and contamination (dust, fibres) better when fixed to a rigid surface.
   - The loop side degrades faster if it is the exposed surface.

3. **Aesthetics and tactility**
   - Loop Velcro on the pad feels cleaner and more “finished” when you are holding or repositioning it.
   - Hook Velcro exposed on a pad edge tends to feel cheap and aggressive.

4. **Failure mode**
   - If the pad is removed temporarily, a hook patch left on the folder is less likely to collect lint than a loop patch would be to lose grip over time.

**Exception:**  
If the notepad will *never* be removed and you want the folder to remain “soft” when empty, you could reverse it—but that is the less robust configuration.

**Recommendation:**  
Hook on folder, loop on notepad.
