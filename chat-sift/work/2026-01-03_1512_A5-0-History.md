## **Layer A5.0 – History (Personal Background & Long-Form Context)**

**Purpose**  
To capture longer-term personal history that provides background, continuity, and context to your present circumstances, without legal framing, justification, or evidential intent.

This layer supports understanding **how you arrived here**, not **what is being alleged** or **how events are argued**.

---

### **What This Layer Is For**

Use A5.0 to record:

- **Life background**  
  Family dynamics, upbringing, formative experiences, long-running patterns.

- **Psychological and emotional history (non-clinical)**  
  Long-standing traits, vulnerabilities, coping styles, stress responses, attachment patterns.

- **Relationship history (high-level)**  
  Repeating dynamics, boundaries, ruptures, reconciliations—without litigating individual incidents.

- **Work, identity, and role history**  
  Career arcs, periods of stability vs disruption, identity shifts, burnout, loss of structure.

- **Long-term stressors**  
  Chronic pressures, cumulative strain, unresolved issues that pre-date the incident.

- **Continuities and patterns**  
  “This has shown up before,” “This is not new,” “This is a repeated theme.”

---

### **What This Layer Is *Not* For**

A5.0 must **not** contain:

- Incident-specific detail (belongs in **A1 – Incident Recollection**)
- Legal analysis, blame, or defence framing
- Police, CPS, or court language
- Attempts to prove, rebut, or justify conduct
- Chronological reconstruction for evidential purposes

If something starts sounding like argument or mitigation, it belongs elsewhere.

---

### **Tone & Writing Rules**

- Plain, reflective language  
- First-person perspective  
- No need for precision or completeness  
- Ambiguity and uncertainty are allowed  
- “This is how it felt / seemed / has been for me” is sufficient

This is a **thinking layer**, not a polished narrative.

---

### **Suggested Internal Structure (Optional)**

You may use headings such as:

- A5.1 – Early background & formative years  
- A5.2 – Long-term emotional patterns  
- A5.3 – Relationship and attachment themes  
- A5.4 – Work, responsibility, and pressure  
- A5.5 – Accumulated stress and erosion  
- A5.6 – What feels consistent over time

Use only what helps—this layer does not need to be symmetrical or complete.
