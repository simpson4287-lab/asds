Below is the **formal definition and operating rules** for the new layer.

---

## **Layer – Incident (Factual Core)**

### **Purpose**
To hold a **clean, neutral, and stable factual account** of the incident as it is alleged to have occurred, without interpretation, argument, or personal context.

This layer acts as the **single reference spine** for the event itself.  
All other layers may reference this layer, but **this layer must not reference them**.

---

### **What this layer contains**
Only **verifiable or declarative facts**, expressed in plain language:

- **Date(s)** of the incident  
- **Time(s)** (exact if known, approximate if not)  
- **Location(s)**  
- **Sequence of events** in chronological order  
- **Actions taken** (by you and others)  
- **What was present or shown** (e.g. CCTV exists, interview occurred)

Each statement should be capable of being prefixed with:
> “It is recorded that…”  
or  
> “On [date], at [location]…”

---

### **What this layer must NOT contain**
Strict exclusions apply:

- No emotions, stress, fear, or mental state  
- No justification, explanation, or mitigation  
- No opinions, beliefs, or assumptions  
- No analysis of evidence  
- No comparison with police versions  
- No hindsight correction  
- No legal framing or defence strategy  

If a sentence answers **“why”** rather than **“what happened”**, it does not belong here.

---

### **Tone & Style Rules**
- Third-person or neutral first-person factual style  
- Short, declarative sentences  
- Chronological ordering only  
- No adjectives unless strictly descriptive (e.g. “red car,” not “dangerous car”)  
- No rhetorical language  

---

### **Change Control**
- This layer should be **rarely edited**  
- Amend only to correct:
  - Dates
  - Times
  - Locations
  - Ordering errors
- No expansion based on later reflection unless the fact itself was missing

Version changes here should be treated as **high-impact**.

---

### **Relationship to Other Layers**
- **Feeds into:**  
  - Evidence & Documentation  
  - Investigation & Interview  
  - Charges & Court Process  
  - Defence (indirectly)

- **Does not absorb from:**  
  - Mental Health  
  - Personal recollection  
  - Strategy or argument layers  

Those layers may explain or contextualise this one, but **this layer remains untouched by them**.

---

### **Recommended Internal Structure**
When you are ready to populate it:

1. **Header**
   - Incident reference
   - Date range

2. **Chronological Record**
   - Entry 1
   - Entry 2
   - Entry 3

3. **Known Materials**
   - Interview occurred (yes/no, date)
   - CCTV exists (yes/no)
   - Police attendance (yes/no)

No appendices. No commentary.
