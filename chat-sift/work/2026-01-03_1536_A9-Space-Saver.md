## **Layer A9 – Spacer / Saver Layer**

### **Purpose**
Layer A9 functions as a **structural buffer and holding layer** within Project A. It exists to temporarily contain material that is **not yet ready**, **not yet classified**, or **not yet contextually anchored** to another layer—without forcing premature decisions or disrupting the integrity of established layers.

This layer protects momentum when material arrives faster than it can be properly processed.

---

### **What This Layer Supports**
- Unsorted notes, fragments, or thoughts awaiting clarification  
- Draft text that may later be refined or relocated  
- Partial recollections or ideas that feel relevant but lack certainty  
- Overflow content created during high-load or time-pressured periods  
- Placeholders for documents or reflections expected later  

---

### **What This Layer Does *Not* Do**
- It is **not** a source of evidence  
- It is **not** relied upon for accuracy, completeness, or chronology  
- It is **not** referenced by other layers  
- It does **not** carry legal, factual, or analytical weight  

Nothing in A9 is assumed to be final, correct, or stable.

---

### **Rules for Use**
- Content may be raw, incomplete, or speculative  
- No requirement for structure, polish, or consistency  
- Items should be dated where practical, but this is optional  
- Material may remain indefinitely if no clear destination emerges  

When clarity improves, content may be **promoted** to another layer or **retired** without consequence.

---

### **Why This Layer Exists (Design Rationale)**
Project A already carries emotional, cognitive, and contextual load. A9 prevents:
- Forced categorisation under pressure  
- Accidental contamination of reflective or baseline layers  
- Loss of potentially useful material due to overload or uncertainty  

It enables continuation without decision paralysis.

---

### **Relationship to Other Layers**
- A9 feeds **into** other layers only when deliberately moved  
- Other layers do **not** feed back into A9  
- A9 stands outside version-locking expectations  

Think of A9 as a **staging area**, not a record.

---

### **One-Line Definition**
**Layer A9 is a non-binding holding space for material that exists but is not yet ready to belong anywhere else.**
