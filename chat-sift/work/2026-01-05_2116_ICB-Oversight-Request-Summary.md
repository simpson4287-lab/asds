**Contemporaneous** means **created, recorded, or occurring at the same time as the events being described**, or very shortly thereafter.

In governance, clinical, and legal contexts, *contemporaneous records* are those made **during or immediately after events**, while details were fresh and before later reflection, interpretation, or dispute. They are generally regarded as more reliable than retrospective accounts because they reflect what was known, said, and done **at the time**.

In practical terms:
- A **contemporaneous note** is written during or soon after an appointment or interaction.  
- A **contemporaneous record** captures decisions, communications, or observations as they occurred, not reconstructed later.  
- The term distinguishes evidence based on **real-time documentation** from hindsight or later reinterpretation.
