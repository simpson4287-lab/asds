## **Project A – Documents**

**Purpose**  
To act as the *document repository* for Project A, holding all written materials that arise from, support, or are produced within Project A, without turning them into evidence or procedural artefacts.

This layer exists to separate **writing** from **thinking**.

---

### **What this layer supports**

- Draft documents written *from* Project A material  
- Structured reflections prepared for:
  - Therapists  
  - Psychologists  
  - Medical professionals  
  - Personal understanding  
- Personal narratives that may later be refined, extracted, or selectively reused  
- Internal summaries that translate raw logs into coherent written form  
- Versioned drafts that are not yet safe or appropriate for Project B  

Examples:
- Personal statements (draft / exploratory)
- Narrative summaries derived from A1 or A2
- Reflective letters (never sent)
- Draft explanations written to understand events, not to defend them
- Therapist-facing summaries that are not medico-legal documents

---

### **What this layer does NOT support**

- Evidence
- Police or court documents
- Anything relied upon procedurally
- Finalised statements intended for submission
- Documents copied directly into Project B

If a document becomes *procedural*, it must migrate to Project B and be re-authored there.

---

### **Relationship to other Project A layers**

- **A1 – Incident Recollection**  
  → Raw memory input

- **A2 – Mental Health Circumstances**  
  → Context and internal state

- **Project A – Documents (this layer)**  
  → Structured writing derived from A1/A2  
  → Interpretation, synthesis, narrative shaping  
  → Still non-procedural

This layer is a **buffer zone** between raw reflection and formal case material.

---

### **Governance rules**

- Drafts are allowed to be imperfect  
- Revisions are allowed  
- Contradictions are allowed  
- No pressure for legal precision  
- No obligation for consistency with Project B  
- Clear labelling: *“Project A – Draft / Non-Procedural”*

---

### **Why this layer matters**

- Prevents contamination of Project B  
- Reduces anxiety about “getting it right first time”  
- Allows healthy processing without procedural risk  
- Gives you a controlled space to *write*, not just log
