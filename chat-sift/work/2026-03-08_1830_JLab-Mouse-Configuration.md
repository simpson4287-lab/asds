Assuming you mean a **JLab mouse**, the setup is usually very straightforward.

For the **USB dongle / 2.4 GHz** mode, plug the little USB receiver into your computer, switch the mouse on, and it should auto-connect. JLab’s manuals for the GO and JBuds mice describe the 2.4 GHz connection as automatic once the dongle is inserted and the mouse is powered on. citeturn1search10turn1search14

For **Bluetooth**, do this:
1. Turn the mouse on.
2. Press the connection button to switch to one of the Bluetooth slots.
3. Press and hold that button until the light flashes to enter pairing mode.
4. On your PC, Mac, iPad, or phone, open Bluetooth settings and select the JLab mouse from the list. JLab’s support page says the JBuds mouse appears as **“JLab JBuds Mouse”** when pairing. citeturn1search5turn1search14

- Many JLab mice support **multiple connection modes**, such as one 2.4 GHz link plus one or two Bluetooth device slots, so you can switch between devices. citeturn1search14turn1search16
- The **DPI button** changes cursor sensitivity, typically cycling through several levels such as 1200, 1600, 2000, and 2400 DPI on supported models. citeturn1search14turn1search16

- Replace or recharge the battery if applicable.
- Move the USB dongle to another USB port.
- Remove the mouse from your device’s Bluetooth list, then pair it again.
