## Sustainability Guardrails

### Principle  
**Health precedes usefulness.**  
Sustained contribution is impossible without physiological and cognitive stability.

### Rules  
- If stress accumulates, exposure is reduced **early**, not after failure signals.  
- Sleep quality, irritability, and rumination are monitored as **leading indicators**, not afterthoughts.  
- Unresolved emotional load is **not transported home**.  
- Cognitive bandwidth is treated as a **finite asset** and protected deliberately.

### Silent Behaviour Change  
- Notice bodily tension as an **early warning**, not background noise.  
- Step back **one interaction sooner** than instinct suggests.  
- End each day **cleanly** (no open emotional or cognitive loops).
