### What makes it a **polo**
A garment is a polo if it has **all** of the following:
- A **fold-down collar**
- A **buttoned placket** (usually 2–3 buttons)
- Typically **piqué or structured knit fabric**
- Slightly more tailored than a T-shirt

### What makes it a **T-shirt**
- **No collar**
- **No buttons**
- Crew neck or V-neck only
- Jersey fabric, casual cut

### Why listings get this wrong
Many retailers incorrectly label polos as “T-shirts” because:
- They are short-sleeved
- They want to appear in broader search results
- Internal category tagging is sloppy

### What to do before buying
Check **one of these** in the product description:
- “**Polo collar**”
- “**Button placket**”
- “**Piqué cotton**”
- Product code or category explicitly saying **polo**

If the **photos clearly show a collar and buttons**, it *is* a polo regardless of what the title says.
