## **Layer A5.7 – Occupational Health**

**Purpose**  
To capture all material relating specifically to occupational health assessment, workplace capability, functional impact, and employer-facing health processes, without conflating this with clinical diagnosis, legal argument, or personal reflection.

This layer sits **adjacent to but distinct from** clinical mental-health layers (A5.2–A5.6) and from any employment or legal dispute documentation that may exist elsewhere.

---

### **What this layer supports**

- Occupational Health (OH) assessments and reports  
- Fitness-for-work opinions (temporary or ongoing)  
- Functional impact of health on work tasks (concentration, stamina, interaction, stress tolerance)  
- Recommended adjustments or restrictions  
- Return-to-work plans, phased duties, or modified hours  
- Employer-requested health questionnaires or summaries  
- Your own contemporaneous notes on OH interactions (factual, not emotive)

---

### **What this layer does *not* support**

- Clinical diagnosis reports (these live in A5.2–A5.6)  
- Legal strategy, grievance drafting, or employment disputes  
- Personal emotional processing or reflective journaling  
- Re-interpretation of OH conclusions after the fact

This layer is **functional and descriptive**, not argumentative.

---

### **Typical contents**

- OH referral letters (incoming or outgoing)  
- OH clinician reports and summaries  
- Capability or fitness statements  
- Adjustment recommendations (e.g. workload, environment, hours)  
- Correspondence confirming OH outcomes  
- Timeline notes (dates of referral, assessment, outcome)

---

### **Tone and handling rules**

- Neutral, professional, and factual  
- Preserve original wording where possible  
- No retrospective reinterpretation  
- Separate facts from your own clarifying notes (clearly labelled)

---

### **Relationship to other Project A layers**

- **Feeds from:**  
  - A5.2–A5.6 (clinical context only, not re-analysed)  
- **Does not feed into:**  
  - Defence, court, or employer challenge layers  
- **Acts as:**  
  - A boundary layer translating health into *workplace function only*

---

### **Versioning**

- Follows Project A versioning convention (A5.7.0 baseline, then step-locked updates if revised alongside other A5 layers)
