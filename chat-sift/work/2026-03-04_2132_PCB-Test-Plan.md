## 5. Detailed test procedures and acceptance criteria

### T01–T03 Package compatibility (TEC + characterisation)
**Purpose:** Demonstrate both package solutions are physically/electrically supported. fileciteturn1file9  
- **T01 Mechanical fit (TEC):** verify mounting, alignment features, keepouts.
- **T02 Mechanical fit (Char):** verify fit via dedicated headboard or interposer.
- **T03 Continuity mapping:** pin-to-net continuity check for both package types (direct or via interposer).  
**Pass:** No mechanical interference; continuity matches controlled pin-mapping.

### T04–T05 Chamber mounting: thermal + electrical contact, illumination unobstructed
**Purpose:** Validate adequate thermal/electrical contact without blocking illumination. fileciteturn1file9  
- **T04 Thermal stability:** with cold finger, log device temp stability at representative setpoints (define “adequate” as stability and settling per program needs).
- **T05 Optical keepout check:** confirm clamps/cables/PCB features do not shadow the active area.  
**Pass:** Temperature stable to required tolerance; no vignetting/shadowing in illumination.

### T06 Obsolescence check by PDR (process gate)
**Purpose:** Confirm lifecycle/obsolescence report exists for early-fixed components. fileciteturn1file9  
**Pass:** Report covers all early-locked components, with risk rating + mitigation, and is available as PDR artifact.

### T07 ESD + physical storage for multiple boards/fixtures
**Purpose:** Confirm storage solution exists for headboards/breakouts. fileciteturn1file9  
**Pass:** ESD-safe storage (shielding + mechanical protection) + labeling scheme (board/rev/status).

---

### T08–T09 Power-on safety: “everything at zero”
**Purpose:** On initial power application, all supplies/biases/clocks = **0 V ±0.1 V**. fileciteturn1file1  
- **T08 No-software power-up:** power the PCB with control interface inactive; measure all bias rails + VH/VL rails at DMM test points.
- **T09 Clock pins safe:** scope representative CCD clock outputs / driver outputs: confirm no toggling and parked at 0 ±0.1 V.  
**Pass:** All measured nodes within ±0.1 V and no unintended activity.

---

### T10–T11 Clock grouping compliance
**Purpose:** Ensure clocks are mapped to the correct VH/VL group sets. fileciteturn1file5  
- **T10 Group mapping audit:** confirm each clock output uses the intended group rails (Image/Memory/Buffer/Register/φR/DG/φDC).
- **T11 Cross-coupling check:** adjust VH/VL of one group; confirm others do not move materially.  
**Pass:** Correct mapping; rail changes are isolated within defined limits.

### T12 Clock high rails: +5 V to +15 V, ±0.1 V
**Purpose:** Validate accuracy and Rameses controllability. fileciteturn1file14  
Procedure:
- For each VH rail: set 5.0 / 7.5 / 10.0 / 12.5 / 15.0 V in Rameses.
- Measure at rail test point under (a) idle and (b) worst-case clock switching.  
**Pass:** |Vmeas − Vset| ≤ 0.1 V at all points.

### T13 Clock low rails: –2 V to +2 V, ±0.1 V
Same as T12, but for VL rails across –2 / –1 / 0 / +1 / +2 V. fileciteturn1file14  
**Pass:** |Vmeas − Vset| ≤ 0.1 V under idle and worst-case switching.

### T14–T17 Slew-rate control (3 ns to 150 ns)
**Purpose:** Demonstrate programmable rise/fall times (10–90%) controlled in Rameses. fileciteturn1file13  
- **T14 Slew setting enumeration:** list available slew modes/steps per clock/group in Rameses.
- **T15 Edge measurement method validation:** use defined measurement tap/port and defined load condition.
- **T16 Slew range test:** at a representative 10 V swing, measure 10–90% rise and fall across settings; confirm coverage of 3…150 ns envelope (or document defined condition if 3 ns only achievable under light load).
- **T17 Repeatability:** repeat on multiple channels and multiple boards.  
**Pass:** Slew settings demonstrably change edge rate and span the required envelope under the agreed measurement condition.

### T18–T19 Frequency and tick resolution
**Purpose:** Prove required frequencies per group, variable within ranges, and tick granularity enabling 24 MHz 3-phase. fileciteturn1file6  
- **T18 Frequency capability:** verify each group achieves max required:
  - Image: 1 MHz / 4 MHz / 24 MHz
  - Memory: 1 MHz / 4 MHz
  - Buffer: 1 MHz
  - Register + φR: 50 kHz / 375 kHz
  - DG/φDC: timed pulses (verify pulse placement/width)  
- **T19 Tick resolution:** demonstrate minimum time-step ≤7 ns (or equivalent) by measuring edge placement granularity at 3.3 V timing outputs.  
**Pass:** Max frequencies achieved; timing quantisation ≤7 ns (or better).

---

### T20–T23 Video gain chain (x1 to x24) + full-well headroom
**Purpose:** Ensure selectable gains cover x1…x24 and x1 supports >400 ke- without saturation. fileciteturn1file0  
- **T20 Gain step verification:** for each gain setting, inject known input stimulus (or equivalent CCD pattern) and compute gain ratio.
- **T21 Gain accuracy/stability:** repeat over time/temperature as required.
- **T22 Headroom at x1:** demonstrate no clipping at ADC/output for a stimulus representing >400 ke- equivalent full well.
- **T23 Noise mode at x24:** confirm x24 enables low-signal/noise/dark current measurements without quantisation dominating.  
**Pass:** Gain range achieved; x1 no saturation at full-well condition; x24 supports intended noise testing.

### T24–T26 AC coupling RC suitability + tunability (50 kHz & 375 kHz)
**Purpose:** Ensure OS settling/droop does not affect measured signal above read noise; allow RC variability by design. fileciteturn1file2  
- **T24 Baseline droop at 50 kHz:** capture waveform; compute droop/settling error at sampling instant.
- **T25 Baseline droop at 375 kHz:** repeat at 375 kHz.
- **T26 Tuning validation:** verify change in RC via (a) parallel cap stuffing options or other allowed mechanism; confirm improvement/coverage.  
**Pass:** Settling/droop errors are below the read-noise threshold defined by program, at both rates; tunability demonstrated.

---

### T27–T29 Scope card + Rameses OS image viewing
**Purpose:** Demonstrate the intended acquisition methodology works and includes OS. fileciteturn1file0  
- **T27 Interface bring-up:** verify video output + required timing markers (PIXCLK/LINE/FRAME or equivalent) are present.
- **T28 Rameses image reconstruction:** capture a frame, confirm OS is present and correctly located in Image Viewer.
- **T29 Repeatability:** repeat at gain x1 and x24.  
**Pass:** Rameses reconstructs stable images with OS; consistent across gain modes.

---

### T30–T33 Bias rails range + accuracy (±0.1 V)
**Purpose:** Validate SS/RD/OD/DD/DDM/OG ranges and accuracy, plus permitted commoning. fileciteturn1file14  
- **T30 SS (0–12 V):** sweep setpoints; DMM verify ±0.1 V.
- **T31 RD (0–25 V), OD (0–35 V), DD/DDM (0–30 V):** sweep; verify ±0.1 V.
- **T32 OG (–2 to +10 V):** sweep; verify ±0.1 V including crossing 0 V.
- **T33 Commoning check:** if OGA/RDA/ODA commoned, verify pin-level equality and absence of unintended drops.  
**Pass:** All rails meet range and ±0.1 V at the measurement point.

### T34–T36 External bias injection (Molex) for RD/OD/SS
**Purpose:** Prove external low-noise supplies can be injected safely, without back-feeding internal sources. fileciteturn1file0  
- **T34 Selection mechanism:** verify jumpers/OR-ing method clearly selects internal vs external.
- **T35 Backfeed test:** apply external rail while internal generator disabled; confirm no reverse drive.
- **T36 Noise improvement demonstration:** measure rail noise (FFT/time) and/or image impact with external supply.  
**Pass:** External injection works, safe isolation confirmed, and measurable noise benefit demonstrated (where applicable).

---

### T37–T38 Test access (DMM + oscilloscope)
**Purpose:** Demonstrate test points/ports exist and are usable. fileciteturn1file1  
- **T37 DMM access audit:** each rail has labeled TP + nearby GND, safe spacing for HV rails.
- **T38 Scope access audit:** representative clock/video ports capture correctly without destabilising operation.  
**Pass:** All required rails/waveforms are directly measurable with standard instruments.

---

### T39–T42 Sample tests enablement (headboard and/or breakouts)
**Purpose:** Ensure the programme’s sample characterisation tests are possible. fileciteturn1file12  
- **T39 Output impedance method:** verify video chain disconnect + injection/measurement port exists; demonstrate measurement on dummy load or CCD.
- **T40 Inter-electrode capacitance:** verify isolation links/jumpers to detach driver and connect LCR breakout.
- **T41 Electrode-to-substrate capacitance:** as above, with SS reference and guard.
- **T42 Output amplifier bandwidth:** measure frequency response/step response; de-embed as needed.  
**Pass:** Headboard provides required isolation/access, and breakouts (if needed) enable valid measurements.

---

### T43–T45 Static and dynamic CCD power measurement
**Purpose:** Prove capability to measure static and dynamic power (shunt method acceptable). fileciteturn1file10  
- **T43 Shunt/readback validation:** confirm shunt locations, Kelvin sense, and measurement access.
- **T44 Static power:** clocks off, biases on; compute P = V×I per rail and total.
- **T45 Dynamic power:** compare clocks idle vs active readout; compute incremental power.  
**Pass:** Method yields repeatable power numbers; dynamic delta measurable.

### T46–T48 Automated output waveform metrics (many-device throughput)
**Purpose:** Demonstrate automated measurement of:
DC reference level, reset feedthrough amplitude, offset level, reference settling time, signal settling time, reference/signal durations. fileciteturn1file12

- **T47 Metric extraction:** run algorithm on captured waveforms; log results.
- **T48 Spot validation:** cross-check a subset manually on a scope to validate the automated metrics. fileciteturn1file10  
**Pass:** Automated pipeline computes all metrics reliably and repeatably; manual spot checks agree within defined tolerance.

# PCB Test Plan (Simplified)

## 1. Objective
Verify that the PCB meets all functional requirements for operating and characterising the CCD device, including:

- Safe power-up behaviour  
- Correct bias and clock voltages  
- Programmable clock timing and slew  
- Video output and gain performance  
- Data acquisition via Rameses  
- Measurement capability (power, impedance, waveform metrics)  
- Mechanical compatibility with test packages

---

# 2. Test Equipment

Minimum equipment:

- Bench power supplies
- 6½ digit DMM
- Oscilloscope (≥1 GHz recommended)
- Signal generator (optional)
- PC running **Rameses**
- Scope capture card
- Thermal chamber / cold finger setup
- ESD workstation

---

# 3. Test Stages

Testing is performed in **six stages**.

1. Mechanical checks  
2. Safe power-up verification  
3. Bias rail verification  
4. Clock and timing verification  
5. Video chain verification  
6. Measurement and characterisation capability

---

# 4. Mechanical and Interface Tests

### 4.1 Package compatibility
Verify the PCB supports both device package options:

- TEC package
- Characterisation package

Confirm:

- Mechanical fit
- Correct connector alignment
- Electrical continuity to all pins

### 4.2 Chamber mounting
Confirm the system:

- Provides good **thermal contact** to the cold finger
- Maintains correct **electrical grounding**
- Does not obstruct **illumination of the CCD**

### 4.3 ESD storage
Verify the design includes a safe method to store:

- Headboards
- Breakout boards

in ESD-safe containers.

---

# 5. Safe Power-Up Tests

### 5.1 Default power condition
When the board powers on:

- All **bias voltages = 0 V ±0.1 V**
- All **clock outputs = 0 V ±0.1 V**

Measure using a DMM.

### 5.2 Clock inactivity
Using an oscilloscope verify:

- No clocks toggle until enabled by control software.

---

Measure all programmable bias rails and confirm they operate within range and accuracy.

| Rail | Range |
|-----|------|
| SS | 0–12 V |
| RD | 0–25 V |

| OG | –2 to +10 V |

Acceptance criteria:

- Voltage accuracy **±0.1 V**
- Stable under load

### External bias input
Verify external supplies can be injected for:

- RD
- OD
- SS

and confirm there is no back-feeding into the internal supply.

---

# 7. Clock System Verification

### 7.1 Clock rail ranges

Verify programmable clock rails:

| Rail | Range |
|----|----|
| VH | 5–15 V |
| VL | –2 to +2 V |

Accuracy requirement:

**±0.1 V**

---

### 7.2 Slew rate control

Verify programmable edge rates from:

**3 ns → 150 ns**

Measure 10-90% rise/fall times on representative clock outputs.

---

### 7.3 Clock frequency capability

Verify each clock group can operate at required speeds:

| Clock group | Frequency |
|---|---|
| Image clocks | up to 24 MHz |
| Memory clocks | up to 4 MHz |

Confirm timing resolution of approximately:

**7 ns tick resolution**

---

# 8. Video Output Verification

Verify programmable gain settings:

**x1 to x24**

Confirm gain ratios using a known test signal.

---

### 8.2 Full-well capability
At **gain x1**, verify the video chain can handle a signal equivalent to:

**>400 ke-**

without clipping.

---

### 8.3 AC coupling behaviour

- Settles correctly at **50 kHz**
- Settles correctly at **375 kHz**

Confirm RC values can be adjusted if required.

---

# 9. Data Acquisition Verification

### 9.1 Scope card operation
Verify the video output can be captured using the **scope card**.

### 9.2 Rameses integration
Confirm the system can:

- Display images

---

# 10. Measurement Capability

- Dynamic power during readout

using the implemented current-sense method.

---

### 10.2 Test access

- All bias rails

for both **DMM** and **oscilloscope** probing.

### 10.3 Device characterisation tests

- Output impedance
