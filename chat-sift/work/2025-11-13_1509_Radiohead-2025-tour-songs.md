[M18] AI (2025-11-13 15:19):
A **marionette** is a type of **puppet controlled by strings**.

### ⭐ What it is
- A figure (often a person or animal shape)  
- Usually made of wood, plastic, or fabric  
- **Moved by strings or wires** attached to the head, hands, feet, and sometimes joints  
- These strings are controlled from above by a puppeteer using a **control bar** or frame

### ⭐ What makes marionettes unique
- They move in a very **lifelike, graceful or exaggerated** way because each limb can be controlled independently  
- They’re used in:
  - Puppet theatre
  - Films
  - Traditional performances (e.g., in Italy, Czech Republic, and France)
  - Symbolic art (e.g., “being someone’s marionette” meaning being controlled)

### ⭐ Quick example
Think of the puppets in *The Sound of Music* (“The Lonely Goatherd”), or the classic wooden puppets in old puppet theatres — those are marionettes.
