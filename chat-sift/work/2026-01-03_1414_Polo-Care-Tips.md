[M2] AI (2026-01-03 14:14):
Looking after an expensive long-sleeve polo is primarily about **reducing mechanical stress, heat exposure, and wash frequency**. Your instinct about using an undershirt is sound, and it is one of the most effective preservation strategies.

---

## 1. Wearing an undershirt — strongly recommended

Yes, wearing an undershirt is a **very good idea**, particularly for premium polos made from fine cotton, merino, or blends.

**Benefits:**
- Absorbs sweat, oils, and deodorant residue before they reach the polo
- Reduces odour retention in the fabric
- Extends time between washes without compromising hygiene
- Preserves collar shape and colour intensity

**Best undershirt choices:**
- Thin cotton or cotton-modal blend
- Lightweight merino (excellent odour control)
- Close-fitting, short-sleeve or sleeveless to avoid sleeve bunching
- Neutral colours (grey or nude under light polos)

Avoid thick undershirts; bulk increases friction and can distort fit.

---

## 2. Washing frequency — less is more

You do **not** need to wash after every wear if:
- You wore an undershirt
- The polo is not visibly soiled
- No strong odour is present

**Guideline:**
- With undershirt: wash every **3–5 wears**
- Without undershirt: wash every **1–2 wears**

Between wears, hang the polo to air for several hours.

---

## 3. Correct washing method (critical)

When washing is needed:

- Turn inside out
- Button the placket
- Cold wash (30 °C max; 20 °C preferred)
- Gentle or hand-wash cycle
- Mild liquid detergent (no optical brighteners)
- **No fabric softener** (it weakens fibres)

If possible, use a **mesh laundry bag** to reduce abrasion.

---

## 4. Drying — never rush this

- **Do not tumble dry**
- Gently reshape while damp
- Lay flat or hang on a padded hanger
- Keep out of direct heat or sunlight

Heat is the fastest way to destroy collars and cuffs.

---

## 5. Storage between wears

- Fold rather than hang for long-term storage (prevents shoulder stretch)
- Keep collars flat
- Avoid overcrowded wardrobes
- Use breathable garment bags if storing for months

---

## 6. Spot-clean instead of full washes

If the collar or cuffs need attention:
- Dab with cool water and a drop of mild detergent
- Gently work with fingers
- Rinse and air dry

This dramatically reduces full wash cycles.

---

## Bottom line

**Yes — wearing an undershirt is one of the best things you can do.**  
Combined with cooler washes and air drying, it can easily **double the lifespan** of an expensive polo.
