## **A8 – Resulting Output of Project**

**Status:** New Layer  
**Purpose:** To capture the *consolidated outputs* that emerge from Project A, without re-opening or re-interpreting the underlying material.

### **What A8 Supports**
This layer is the **end-product layer** of Project A. It exists to hold outcomes that have been formed *after* reflection, organisation, and synthesis across prior layers.

Typical inclusions:
- Personal summaries derived from multiple A-layers  
- Clarified narratives you are satisfied with (not raw memory)  
- Personal insight statements (e.g. “What this process has shown me”)  
- Consolidated timelines or overviews created for *your own understanding*  
- Personal readiness notes (e.g. “What I am clear on now”)  
- Any material that feels *resolved*, not exploratory

### **What A8 Does NOT Support**
- Raw recollections or memory fragments (A1/A4)  
- Ongoing mental health context or symptom logging (A5.x)  
- Draft thinking, uncertainty, or hypothesis testing  
- Legal framing, argumentation, or defence construction  
- Evidence, documents, or factual verification

If something still feels “in progress,” it does **not** belong in A8.

### **Key Characteristics**
- **Reflective, not reactive**  
- **Summative, not granular**  
- **Stable** (minimal revision expected)  
- **Personal** (not written for court, professionals, or third parties)

### **Relationship to Other Layers**
- Draws *from* A1–A7  
- Does not feed back into them  
- Does not override earlier content  
- Exists as a closing layer, not a control layer

### **Mental Model**
If Project A is “thinking out loud safely,”  
**A8 is: _“What I now know, having done that thinking.”_**
