## **Layer Purpose**
**Document Transcription** is the controlled intake and conversion point for source materials that exist only as images, scans, photographs, handwritten notes, or non-editable formats.

Its sole function is to convert material into **accurate, verbatim, editable text** without interpretation, analysis, or restructuring.

---

## **What This Layer Supports**
- Transcription of:
  - Handwritten notes (journals, logs, annotations)
  - Photographs of documents
  - Scanned PDFs or screenshots
  - Forms, letters, records, or notebooks
- Preservation of:
  - Original wording
  - Line breaks and paragraph structure (where visible)
  - Spelling, grammar, and punctuation as written
  - Ambiguity, omissions, or unclear text (explicitly flagged)
- Output formats:
  - Clean plain text
  - Word-ready formatting (headings, paragraphs, lists where visually obvious)
  - Optional page/section markers for traceability

---

## **What This Layer Does *Not* Do**
- No summarising
- No correcting or “cleaning up” language
- No legal framing, interpretation, or contextualisation
- No merging with other layers
- No assumptions about intent or meaning

If meaning, relevance, or implications matter, that belongs **after transcription**, in the appropriate analytical or procedural layer.

---

## **Operating Rules**
- **Verbatim first**: accuracy over readability  
- **Unclear content** is explicitly marked (e.g. `[illegible]`, `[uncertain word]`)
- **One document = one transcription output**
- Transcribed content can then be *copied forward* into other layers, but this layer remains the source of truth

---

## **Recommended Naming Convention**
To align with your existing structure and version control:

```
DT_[LayerRef]_[Date]_SourceDescription
```

Example:
```
DT_L2_030126_HandwrittenNotes_Page1
DT_L1_030126_CustodyRecord_Photo
```
