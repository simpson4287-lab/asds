## **Project A – Layer A4: Mental Health Circumstances (Personal Context)**

### **Purpose of Layer A4**
This layer exists to capture your **mental health state, vulnerabilities, and internal context** around the relevant period, without filtering it through legal standards, hindsight, or external judgment.

It is explicitly **non-forensic**, **non-defensive**, and **non-evidential**.

This layer answers one question only:

> *“What was happening internally for me at the time?”*

---

### **What This Layer Supports**
Layer A4 may include:

- Mental state in the weeks and months leading up to the incident  
- Psychological stressors (acute and chronic)
- Emotional regulation difficulties
- Fatigue, burnout, overwhelm
- Anxiety, low mood, dissociation, shutdown, rumination
- Effects of disrupted sleep, routine, or support
- Impact of ongoing systems stress (healthcare, admin, legal uncertainty)
- Internal responses that would never appear in official records
- Subjective experience of capacity, resilience, and coping
- Retrospective insight **only where it helps you understand yourself**, not to justify or argue

This is the layer where **nuance, contradiction, and uncertainty are allowed**.

---

### **What This Layer Does NOT Support**
This layer must **not** be used for:

- Legal argument or mitigation
- Excuses, justifications, or blame allocation
- Rewriting events to appear more favourable
- Diagnostic claims unless already formally made elsewhere
- Police, CPS, or court-aligned language
- “What I should have done” narratives

If something starts to sound like defence strategy, it belongs in **Project B**, not here.

---

### **Tone and Writing Rules**
- First-person, plain language  
- Reflective, not persuasive  
- No pressure for consistency  
- Emotional honesty over coherence  
- “I felt”, “I struggled”, “I don’t fully understand” are valid  
- Contradictions can coexist  

This is **your internal record**, not a document to be read aloud.

---

### **Recommended Structure (Template)**

You may use this structure, but it is optional:

**A4.1 – Baseline Mental Health Context**  
- Longer-term mental health background  
- Known vulnerabilities or patterns  
- What “normal functioning” looks like for you  

**A4.2 – Pre-Incident Stressors**  
- Accumulating pressures  
- External demands  
- Loss of stability or support  

**A4.3 – Mental State Around the Time**  
- Emotional state  
- Cognitive load and clarity  
- Capacity for decision-making  
- Sense of control vs overwhelm  

**A4.4 – Physical & Environmental Factors**  
- Sleep, exhaustion  
- Health issues  
- Medication, alcohol, substances (if relevant)  
- Environmental pressures  

**A4.5 – Internal Experience (Unfiltered)**  
- Thoughts you would not normally share  
- Fears, loops, shutdowns  
- Emotional reactions that felt disproportionate or confusing  

**A4.6 – Early Reflection (Non-Judgmental)**  
- What you now recognise about that period  
- Without hindsight correction or moral framing
