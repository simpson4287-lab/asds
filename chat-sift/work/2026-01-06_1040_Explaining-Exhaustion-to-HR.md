In contrast, maintaining a single, predictable environment throughout the day supports sustained focus, emotional regulation, and productivity. This adjustment therefore addresses a functional necessity arising from Autism Spectrum traits, rather than convenience or preference.

It is also recognised that this heightened awareness of “what’s next,” while demanding, reflects a conscientious and responsible working style, characterised by maintaining oversight of responsibilities and anticipating downstream impact.
