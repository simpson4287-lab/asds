## **Layer 6.1 – First Hearing & Preparation**

**Status:** Draft (6.1.0)  
**Project:** Project B – Full Case File  
**Purpose:** To consolidate all preparation, decisions, and materials required ahead of the first court hearing, and to capture outcomes immediately following that hearing.

---

### **What this layer supports**

This layer is **procedural and forward-looking**, focused on readiness rather than argument.

It supports:

- Practical preparation for the first court appearance  
- Understanding and documenting procedural options  
- Reducing uncertainty and cognitive load before the hearing  
- Creating a clear handover point between preparation and post-hearing actions  

---

### **Scope and inclusions**

#### **1. Hearing logistics**
- Court name, location, date, and time  
- Mode of hearing (in-person / remote, if applicable)  
- Reporting instructions (arrival time, security, waiting areas)  
- Any correspondence from the court relating to the hearing  

#### **2. Representation status**
- Duty solicitor vs instructed solicitor (if any)  
- Notes from any pre-hearing discussion with legal counsel  
- Confirmation of what representation (if any) will be available on the day  

#### **3. Charge and plea clarity**
- Confirmed charges as listed for the hearing  
- Plea options and implications (plea entered or adjourned)  
- Any advice received regarding plea timing  

*(No argumentation — factual recording only. Substantive defence remains elsewhere.)*

#### **4. Sentencing framework awareness**
- Likely sentencing band / range (high-level understanding only)  
- Factors flagged as potentially relevant (e.g. mitigation reports, mental-health context already documented in earlier layers)  
- Identification of any reports or information the court may request  

#### **5. Preparation checklist**
- Documents to bring (ID, paperwork, court letters)  
- Notes prepared for solicitor or court (if appropriate)  
- Practical considerations (travel, timing, stress management plan for the day)  

#### **6. Questions and decision points**
- Open procedural questions to resolve before the hearing  
- Decisions to be made on the day (e.g. plea entry, adjournment requests)  
- Risks and unknowns identified in advance  

#### **7. Post-hearing capture (to be completed after)**
- What occurred procedurally  
- Plea entered or next steps ordered  
- Dates set, instructions given, or documents requested  
- Immediate follow-up actions required  

---

### **What this layer explicitly does NOT do**

- Does **not** rehearse evidence or defence arguments  
- Does **not** reinterpret earlier factual or mental-health layers  
- Does **not** attempt legal advocacy or strategy beyond preparation  
- Does **not** replace solicitor advice  

Those elements remain contained in their respective layers to preserve clarity and auditability.

---

  - Post-hearing procedural tracking
