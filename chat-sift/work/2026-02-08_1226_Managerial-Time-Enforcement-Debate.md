# Project: Internal Operating Doctrine 2.0  
## Section 0 – Project Charter

### 0.1 Purpose

To formally redesign my professional operating model in response to structural overload, boundary erosion, and misaligned responsibility weighting.

This project establishes a sustainable, defined, and internally governed mode of operation that protects:

- Cognitive bandwidth  
- Professional credibility  
- Emotional stability  
- Long-term optionality  

This is a structural correction, not a reaction.

---

### 0.2 Background Context

Historically, responsibility expanded beyond formal remit, combining:

- Technical delivery
- Coordination oversight
- Administrative load
- Emotional containment of team dynamics

This created role compression and unsustainable executive load without structural safeguards.

Subsequent reallocation occurred without consultative decomposition of responsibilities, resulting in:

- Reduced flexibility
- Increased procedural oversight
- Narrowed trust bandwidth

This charter acknowledges these structural realities without assigning blame.

---

### 0.3 Problem Statement

Unbounded responsibility and implicit role expansion led to:

- Chronic cognitive overextension
- Emotional labour accumulation
- Stress-related health impact
- Identity conflation between contribution and containment

Continuation of prior operating model is non-viable.

---

### 0.4 Project Objective

To implement a self-governed professional model defined by:

- Clear responsibility envelope
- Controlled emotional engagement
- Clean technical excellence
- Reduced invisible labour
- Measurable sustainability

Success = sustained performance without systemic overreach.

---

### 0.5 Constraints

- Organisational structure is outside direct control.
- Leadership style is non-adjustable unless explicitly invited.
- Compensation structure remains constant.
- Role boundaries may not be formally documented.

Therefore:
This model must function independent of structural reform.

---

### 0.6 Non-Negotiables

1. Health precedes usefulness.
2. Responsibility must be defined, not assumed.
3. Additional load requires explicit trade-off.
4. No invisible labour.
5. No emotional over-absorption.

---

### 0.7 Scope

In scope:
- Behavioural boundaries
- Cognitive load management
- Communication calibration
- Response protocols
- Internal narrative regulation

Out of scope:
- Reforming leadership
- Correcting team behaviour
- Reclaiming informal authority
- Proving prior capability

---

### 0.8 Success Metrics (Private)

- Reduced rumination outside work hours.
- Stable energy across weeks.
- No voluntary assumption of undefined responsibilities.
- Deliverables completed without stress accumulation.
- Emotional neutrality in response to micro-control.

---

### 0.9 Governance

Owner: Self  
Review cycle: Monthly  
Escalation trigger: Early signs of overload or resentment  

Amendments allowed only after reflective review — not reactive emotion.
