**"Beckoned"** means **to signal or call someone over with a gesture or movement**, usually using a hand, head, or some subtle motion. It implies inviting or drawing someone toward you.

### In simple terms:
- **Beckoned = gestured to come closer**
- Like when someone **waves you over** or **nods their head for you to follow**

### Examples:
- *She beckoned him into the room with a slight wave.*  
- *The warm light beckoned me inside.*  
- *He stood at the door, quietly beckoning me to follow.*

It can be **literal** (physically signalling someone) or **figurative** (something metaphorically calling you, like music, memories, temptation).
