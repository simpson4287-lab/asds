## **Purpose**

Project B is the **single, authoritative case file** for the real-world legal matter.

It exists to:
- Mirror the case as it exists externally (police, CPS, court)
- Preserve accuracy, traceability, and procedural integrity
- Prevent contamination from memory, emotion, or post-hoc interpretation

Project B is written on the assumption that:
- It may be reviewed by a solicitor
- It may be used to brief counsel
- It may be relied upon to prepare for court

Accordingly, it is factual, controlled, and conservative in tone.

---

1. **Factual primacy**  
   Facts come first. Interpretation is tightly controlled and clearly located.

2. **Layer separation**  
   Each layer has a defined function. Content does not “bleed” between layers.

3. **Document-led**  
   Wherever possible, assertions are backed by documents, recordings, or formal records.

4. **Version discipline**  
   Layers move in lockstep versioning to avoid drift and contradiction.

5. **No self-advocacy tone**  
   The project is not written to persuade emotionally. It is written to withstand scrutiny.
