Short answer: **yes, you can pay it off early, but you will still pay *some* interest — just not all of it.**

Here is how it works in the UK.

---

## 1. What “paying it off early” actually means
You do **not** pay all future interest.

Instead, the lender:
- Calculates your **early settlement figure**
- Removes **most of the future interest**
- May add a **small statutory charge**

You then pay **one lump sum** and the loan ends.

---

## 2. Why you can’t avoid *all* interest
Interest is charged:
- Daily / monthly
- For the time you’ve already had the money

So interest **already incurred** cannot be removed.

What *is* removed is:
- Interest that **would have been charged in future months**

---

## 3. The only extra cost (important)
Under UK consumer credit rules, the lender is allowed to charge:

- **Up to 58 days’ interest** as an early settlement charge  
  (this is standard and legal)

That’s roughly:
- ~2 months of interest
- On your current balance (≈ £11k), that’s about **£110–£130**

No other penalties are allowed.
