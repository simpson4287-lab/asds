- ✅ **Neutral or motivating**:  
  In friendly or informal settings — for example between teammates or friends — *“Come on, get on with it!”* can sound like encouragement or playful impatience, especially if said with a smile or light tone.  
  > e.g. “You’ve got this, just get on with it!”  

- ⚠️ **Potentially rude or dismissive**:

  > e.g. Saying “Get on with it” to someone who’s nervous or explaining something personal could come across as **cold or rude**.
