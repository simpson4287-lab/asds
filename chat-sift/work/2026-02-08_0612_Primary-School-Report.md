## National Curriculum – Core Subjects (Teacher Report)

### **English**
> “Chris can give a detailed oral account of an event or explain with reasons why a particular course of action has been taken. He can describe the qualities of a story or poem which appeal and can explain why he liked it.  
>  
> He can read aloud fluently and is trying hard to improve upon expression. Chris can read silently with sustained concentration. When creating stories he can describe settings and draw the work to a close.  
>  
> Chris has improved his spelling accuracy but should try to use class resources more. He is developing a clear, legible joined-up script.”

---

### **Mathematics**
> “Chris can count, write and use numbers to 100 and above. He will however require further practice with division. He can make realistic estimates and is developing an accurate approach in measure.  
>  
> Chris is reading multiplication tables with increasing confidence and this is helping him in problem solving. He has been able to identify halves and quarters. Chris has recognised and named common 2-D and 3-D shapes and has used and understood right angles.”

---

### **Science**
> “Chris can carry out investigations based on his immediate experience and is able to observe familiar materials and events. With support he can ask questions, suggest ideas and make predictions and is able to compare what he has observed with what he expected.  
>  
> Chris has investigated fuels and energy, colour, light, healthy diet and parts of the body. He has produced complete electrical circuits using switches and bulbs.”

---

## Other Aspects of Learning

### **Technology**
> “Chris has used a range of materials and tools. He designed and built a clever propeller driven vehicle.”

### **Geography**
> “Chris has investigated maps and features used on maps and has located these using co-ordinates.”

### **History**
> “Chris is able to talk about past and present times and explain some differences between them.”

### **Art**
> “Chris has worked with interest on a range of projects stimulated by historical objects or the work of other artists.”

### **Music**
> “Chris has written and interpreted standard musical symbols.”

### **Physical Education**
> “He has improved his skills of throwing, catching and travelling with a ball for invasion, net and striking/fielding games.”
