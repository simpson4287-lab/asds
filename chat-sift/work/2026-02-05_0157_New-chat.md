**What it does *not* do:**
- It does **not** directly increase Elvanse absorption or conversion.
- It will **not compensate** for tolerance, under-sleeping, dehydration, or poor overall nutrition.

3. **Sleep duration/quality:** The strongest modulator of response.

5. **Avoiding heavy acidic intake around dosing** (large amounts of vitamin C/citrus close to dose can slightly reduce absorption).
