1. **Locate the ceiling joists**

   - Fix at **every joist intersection**
   - Pull the plate tight but don’t over-torque and crack plaster

**Why this is best:**  
- Structural strength  
- No ceiling movement later  
- Proper load transfer  
- Meets building-control expectations
