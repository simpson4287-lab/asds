Here are the **easiest ways to check if you’re running Office 365 (now called Microsoft 365)** on Windows:

---

## ✅ **1. Check in any Office app (Word, Excel, Outlook, etc.)**

### **Method A: From the Account page**
1. Open **Word** (or Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook).
2. Click **File** in the top-left.
3. Click **Account** (sometimes **Office Account**).

Now look for:

### **Signs you have Microsoft 365**
- It says **Microsoft 365** under *Product Information*  
  Example text:  
  **“Product Activated – Microsoft 365 Apps for business/personal/family”**
- You see a line like:  
  **“Subscription Product”**
- Updates come through **Current Channel** (subscriptions update monthly).

### **Signs you have a one-time purchase (NOT Microsoft 365)**
- It says something like:
  - **Office 2016**
  - **Office 2019**
  - **Office 2021**
- It says **“Product Activated — Office Home & Student 2021”**
- No mention of subscription.

---

## ✅ **2. Check via Windows Settings**
1. Go to **Settings** → **Apps** → **Installed Apps**
2. Scroll to **Microsoft Office** or **Microsoft 365**

- If it says **Microsoft 365 Apps**, that's the subscription version.
- If it shows specific year versions (e.g., **Office 2019**), then it's not 365.

---

## ✅ **3. Check in your Microsoft Account**
Go to: **https://account.microsoft.com/services**

If you see:

- **Microsoft 365 Personal / Family / Business** with an active status → you have Office 365.

If nothing is listed or you see an expired subscription, you might be using an old standalone Office version.
