Would the sheet metal survive a folded crease?

[M2] AI (2025-12-27 13:03):
Yes—sheet metal will usually survive being folded into a crease, but whether it *survives cleanly* (no cracking, no ugly “white line,” no spring-back that unbends it, and no sharp burrs) depends on four things:

### 1) Material type
- **Stainless steel**: most likely to crack if you try to put in a very tight crease (it work-hardens fast).
- **Mild steel**: generally more forgiving than stainless.
- **Aluminium**: can crease well, but some alloys crack more easily; it also shows a pronounced bend line.

### 2) Thickness
- **Very thin sheet (e.g., ~0.2–0.4 mm)** will crease easily, but it can “oil-can” (warp) and the edge can become razor sharp.
- **Thicker sheet** resists forming and will need a larger bend radius or proper tooling.

### 3) Bend radius (this is the big one)
A “folded crease” implies a **near-zero inside radius**, which is the highest-risk case for cracking—especially in stainless.

Rule of thumb:
- If you bend around *something* (even a small rod), you massively reduce cracking risk.
- A tight fold done by pinching flat between hard edges is where cracks start.

### 4) Grain direction (rolled sheet)
Bending **across the grain** is safer than bending **with the grain**. With grain bends crack more readily.

---

## Practical guidance (so it survives)
If you want a crease but don’t want failure:

1) **Avoid a knife-edge fold.**  
   Put a small radius in it—bend around a drill bit/rod, or use a brake with a sensible nose radius.

2) **Score line? Don’t.**  
   Scoring (scratching a line) makes it *much* more likely to tear/crack at the crease.

3) **If it’s stainless, go gentler.**  
   Do a larger radius first, then tighten gradually rather than trying to “snap” it into a crease.

4) **Expect spring-back.**  
   Stainless springs back more than mild steel; you usually over-bend slightly to hit your final angle.

---

## For your use-case (thin stainless sheet to mount on plastic / planner panel)
If you’re thinking of **0.3 mm stainless**: it will bend, but a *hard crease* can kink and may start micro-cracks at the outer surface if you force a zero-radius fold. If you instead bend it around even a **1–2 mm radius** (a small rod/drill shank), it’s typically fine.
