## **B4 – Context & Mental Health (Formal)**

**Status:** Authoritative supporting context  
**Purpose:** To provide *document-backed clinical and functional context* relevant to the incident and proceedings, without speculation, advocacy, or informal narrative.

### **What this layer supports**
This layer exists to evidence **mental health circumstances as factual context**, not mitigation argument.

It may support:
- Clinical background relevant to **capacity, stress, cognition, or behaviour**
- Evidence of **diagnosis, treatment, impairment, or escalation**
- Professional observations contemporaneous with, or proximate to, the incident
- Formal corroboration of vulnerability or functional impact

### **Permitted content**
Only **formal, attributable material**:
- GP letters or summaries  
- Psychiatric / psychological reports  
- NHS correspondence (appointments, referrals, assessments)  
- Private clinician letters or assessments  
- Medication records (prescribed, dated)  
- Occupational health or functional assessments  
- Crisis team / mental health service notes (where disclosed)  

Documents must be:
- Dated  
- Authored  
- Traceable  
- Unedited (no paraphrasing inside the layer)

### **Explicit exclusions**
This layer must **not** contain:
- Personal recollection or emotional narrative  
- Retrospective interpretation  
- Opinion or argument  
- Self-diagnosis  
- Therapy reflections or journaling  
- “How this felt” descriptions  

Those belong **only** in Project A.

### **Structural rules**
- Materials are **stored, not interpreted**
- No commentary or framing text inside the layer
- Each document logged with:
  - Date  
  - Author / service  
  - Document type  
  - Reference number (if applicable)

### **Relationship to other layers**
- **B3 – Incident (Factual Core):**  
  No cross-editing. B3 remains clinically and emotionally neutral.
- **B5 – Investigation & Interview:**  
  May reference existence of B4 documents but not analyse them.
- **B6 – Charges & Court Process:**  
  May later cite B4 material procedurally (e.g. disclosure, bundle inclusion).

### **Governance principle**
This layer answers only:
> *“What formal mental-health context exists, as evidenced by professionals?”*

It does **not** answer:
> *“What should be concluded from it?”*
