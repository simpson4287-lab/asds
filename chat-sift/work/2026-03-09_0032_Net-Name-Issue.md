Because that wire is **already named something else**.

In your screenshot, the net is showing **`OD_0`** on the wire, and then you’ve also placed **`VDD`** on the same conductor. In Altium, one continuous wire can only end up with **one effective net name**. If two naming objects are present, one wins and the other gets ignored or causes ambiguity.

What I can see:
- the horizontal wire already has **`OD_0`** on it
- you’ve then added **`VDD`** at the right-hand side
- so **`VDD` is not taking over**, because the net is already being identified as `OD_0`

What to do:
1. Click the **`OD_0`** label and see whether it is a **Net Label** or a **Port**.
2. Delete or rename the unwanted naming object.
3. Leave only **one naming source** on that wire.
4. Recompile.

Also check this:
- If `VDD` is slightly **off-grid** and not actually snapped to the wire, it will look attached but won’t electrically name it.
- The quickest test is to **delete `VDD` and place it again**, making sure the hotspot snaps onto the wire end.

So the most likely answer is: **`OD_0` is currently the active net name, not `VDD`.**
