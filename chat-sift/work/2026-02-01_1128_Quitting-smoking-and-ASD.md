## Likely short-term effects (first 3–7 days after stopping)

**Common**
- Irritability, restlessness
- Increased anxiety or low mood
- Difficulty concentrating (“brain fog”)

- Strong urge to self-soothe or stim

- Headache
- Mild dissociation or emotional blunting

---

## How this interacts with an ASD assessment

**What it may influence**
- You may appear more anxious, tense, or fatigued
- Executive function (attention, verbal fluency) may be temporarily reduced
- Masking may be harder → this can actually *reveal* autistic traits more clearly

**What it does NOT affect**
- Developmental history (childhood traits, lifelong patterns)
- Core diagnostic criteria
- Clinician’s ability to diagnose ASD

ASD assessments are based on **lifelong patterns**, not a snapshot mood state.

- Prioritise sleep (even if imperfect)

- Avoid excessive caffeine (amplifies withdrawal anxiety)
