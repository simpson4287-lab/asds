**NICE CG90, NICE NG222, NICE NG87, NICE CG142**

- Persistent or treatment-resistant depression should prompt **review by specialist mental-health services** (NICE CG90, section 1.4; NICE NG222, sections 1.5–1.6).

### 2. Inappropriate Restriction of Access Based on Crisis Behaviour  
**NICE CG90, NICE NG222**

NICE guidance explicitly warns against restricting access to appropriate care based on behaviours that are themselves **symptoms of acute mental illness**.

NICE CG90 and NG222 state that:
- Risk-related or crisis behaviours require **enhanced assessment and support**, not exclusion from services.
- Comorbid substance use in depression should be addressed through **integrated care**, not used as a barrier to assessment.
- Treatment resistance increases the need for **specialist review**, not deferral.

The continued reliance on a brief episode of crisis-driven self-medication as justification for delaying or rejecting psychiatric access is **inconsistent with NICE guidance** and raises significant concerns regarding equitable access to care.
