How do you initiate a sage cleaning cycle 

[M2] AI (2026-05-24 13:03):
For most **Sage / Breville espresso machines**, the “cleaning cycle” means the **backflush cleaning cycle** using a cleaning tablet, not descaling.

General process:

1. **Empty the drip tray** and fill the water tank.
2. Insert the **1-cup filter basket** into the portafilter.
3. Put in the **rubber cleaning disc**.
4. Place **one Sage cleaning tablet** on top of the disc.
5. Lock the portafilter into the group head.
6. Put a container under the portafilter/spouts.
7. Start the cleaning cycle using the machine’s button sequence.

The button sequence depends on the model:

**Barista Express / Express Impress**
Hold **1 CUP + 2 CUP**, then press **POWER**. Keep holding until the cleaning cycle starts.

**Barista Pro**
Go to the menu using the **MENU** button, select **CLEAN CYCLE**, then press/select to start.

**Barista Touch / Touch Impress**
Use the touchscreen:  
**Settings → Cleaning Cycle**  
Then follow the on-screen prompts.

**Dual Boiler**
Usually:  
Turn machine off, then hold **1 CUP + 2 CUP**, then press **POWER** to enter cleaning mode.

The cycle normally runs in pulses for several minutes. When finished, remove the portafilter, rinse the basket/disc, then run a couple of blank shots to flush any cleaner.

If your machine is showing **“Clean Me”**, this is the cycle you want. If it says **“Descale”**, that is a different process.
