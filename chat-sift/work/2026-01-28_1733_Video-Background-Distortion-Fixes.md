## Notes on Terminology

- **Background distortion** in video can mean *shakiness (motion)*, *lens distortion (fisheye/barrel)*, or *visual noise/artifacts*.  
  - *Stabilization tools* smooth out camera motion and shaky backgrounds.  
  - *Lens correction tools* adjust geometric warping caused by wide-angle or action camera lenses.  
  - Dedicated plugins like **Neat Video** focus on visual noise cleanup rather than geometric distortion.
