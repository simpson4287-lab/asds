## **A5.6 — Substance Use Disorder (SUD)**

**Scope:**  
This layer captures *personal context* relating to substance use patterns, impacts, and risk factors, strictly as lived experience and self-reflection. It is **non-forensic, non-diagnostic, and non-legal** in purpose.

**Purpose:**  
To document how substance use has intersected with mental health, stress, functioning, and vulnerability over time, without framing, justification, or defence strategy.

---

### **What this layer supports**

- Personal history of substance use (types, frequency, patterns over time)  
- Periods of escalation, reduction, abstinence, or relapse  
- Triggers and contextual factors (stress, isolation, sleep disruption, emotional overload)  
- Interaction with diagnosed or suspected conditions (e.g. MDD, ADHD, ASD)  
- Subjective effects on mood, cognition, impulse control, memory, or behaviour  
- Self-recognised risks, concerns, or insight (including ambivalence or uncertainty)  
- Harm-reduction efforts or self-initiated controls  
- Reflections on how substance use affected daily functioning or decision-making

---

### **What this layer does NOT support**

- Legal argument or mitigation  
- Police, CPS, or court-facing narrative  
- Medical diagnosis or formal clinical conclusions  
- Quantification intended to “prove” severity  
- Comparison with external evidence (CCTV, interviews, statements)  
- Retrospective justification of events

---

### **Tone and rules for use**

- Plain language only  
- First-person perspective  
- No moral framing (neither minimising nor catastrophising)  
- Uncertainty is allowed (“I’m not sure”, “I don’t remember clearly”)  
- Write once, revise sparingly  
- This layer is *contextual*, not explanatory

---

### **Relationship to other layers**

- **Links to A5.2 / A5.3 / A5.4 / A5.5:** documents interaction with depression, ADHD, ASD  
- **Distinct from A5.1 (Crisis):** focuses on substance patterns, not acute crisis events  
- **Separated from Project B:** content here is *never relied upon* for legal purposes

---

### **Key question this layer answers**

> *“What role, if any, did substance use play in my mental state and functioning as I experienced it?”*
