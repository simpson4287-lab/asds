The Notes app hides this behind a couple of tiny UI rituals, because of course it does.

Here’s the clean way to **Select All** on an iPhone in the **Notes** app:

**Inside a note (editing text):**
1. Tap inside the note so the keyboard appears.
2. **Long-press on a word** until the text selection handles pop up.
3. In the black pop-up menu, tap **Select All**.

If you don’t see it immediately, swipe that pop-up menu left — it likes to tuck useful things off-screen.

A couple of useful wrinkles:
- If the note is long, you may need to tap **Select** first, then **Select All** appears.
- Double-tap selects a word, triple-tap often selects a paragraph, but Apple never fully commits to that behaving consistently.
- There is **no keyboard shortcut** on iPhone like ⌘A unless you’re using a hardware keyboard — in that case, ⌘A works exactly as expected (a rare moment of sanity).

For **selecting multiple notes** (not text):
- Tap **Select** in the top-right of the Notes list.
- Tap the notes you want. There is *no* “Select All” here — this is a design choice, not a bug, and we all pretend to accept it.

This is one of those small examples where Apple optimises for “clean” over “discoverable,” which is elegant until you’re trying to move 800 lines of text before coffee.
