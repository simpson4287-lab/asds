- Charities often provide counselling, peer support, and groups for ADHD, autism, depression, eating disorders, and substance misuse.

### 4. **Private or Low-Cost Psychiatry / Therapy**
- If finances allow, private psychiatrists can give diagnostic clarification, medication reviews, and letters that might support benefits or re-accessing NHS care.
