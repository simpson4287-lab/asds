Here’s the clean mental model for **Nationwide eSavings**.

Nationwide eSavings pays **variable interest**, calculated **daily**, based on **your balance that day**. Many versions of the account have **tiers** (for example: one rate above a certain balance, a lower rate below it).

So when they say *“your interest will drop if you transfer another sum out”*, they mean this:

• If your balance falls **below a tier threshold**, the **interest rate applied going forward** will be lower  
• Nothing retroactive happens  
• No punishment, no claw-back, no time travel

### Do you lose interest already earned?
No.  
Interest you’ve already accrued is safe.

Daily interest is effectively locked in at the end of each day based on that day’s balance. Once earned, it’s yours.

### When does the lower interest start?
From the **day your balance drops**.

Think of it like a light switch, not a rewind button:

• Balance high → higher daily rate  
• Balance drops → lower daily rate from that date onward  
• Interest before that date stays unchanged

### What “period” are we talking about?
Interest is:
• **Calculated daily**
• **Paid monthly** (usually)

So a typical month might look like:
• Days 1–18: higher balance → higher daily interest  
• Days 19–30: lower balance → lower daily interest  
• End of month: they add both parts together and pay it

### What this is *not*
It is not:
• A penalty
• A fee
• A loss of historical interest
• A reset of the month
• A special restriction on withdrawals

It’s simply tiered pricing responding to a lower balance.
