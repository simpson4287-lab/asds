- **Arrest** someone,

The CPS decides whether there’s:
- Enough **evidence** to provide a realistic prospect of conviction, and  
- Whether it’s in the **public interest** to prosecute.

---

However, an officer **can** decide to:
- **Take no further action (NFA)** if there’s clearly not enough evidence or it’s obviously minor.

People often say *“I don’t want to press charges”* (for example, a victim of assault).

- The **victim’s wishes are taken into account**, but they **don’t control** the process.
