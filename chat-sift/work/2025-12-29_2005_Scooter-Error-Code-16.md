Error code **16** on a scooter is **not universal**—its meaning depends entirely on the **manufacturer and model**. Unlike car OBD codes, scooter fault codes are brand-specific.

## Common Meanings of Scooter Error Code 16

### 1. **Throttle Fault (Very Common)**
Seen on many generic Chinese controllers and brands like Xiaomi-derived designs.

Typical causes:
- Throttle hall sensor out of range  
- Throttle not returning to zero at power-on  
- Damaged throttle cable or connector  
- Water ingress in throttle housing

Actions:
- Power off, wait 30 seconds, power on without touching throttle  
- Inspect throttle wiring at handlebar and controller  
- Look for corrosion or broken conductors  
- If persistent, throttle replacement is usually required

---

### 2. **Brake Sensor / E-Brake Fault**
Common on scooters with electronic brake cut-off.

Typical causes:
- Brake lever sensor stuck “on”  
- Magnet misalignment in brake lever  
- Damaged brake sensor wiring

Actions:
- Ensure brake levers fully return  
- Disconnect brake sensor temporarily (for test only)  
- Inspect lever magnets and cabling

---

### 3. **Controller Communication Error**
Seen on some models with display ↔ controller CAN/UART links.

Typical causes:
- Loose display cable  
- Controller internal fault  
- Voltage instability at startup

Actions:
- Reseat display and controller connectors  
- Check battery voltage under load  
- Inspect for water damage

---

### 4. **Battery / BMS Protection Triggered**
Less common but reported on higher-power scooters.

Typical causes:
- Over-current at startup  
- BMS fault or imbalance  
- Low voltage under load

Actions:
- Fully charge battery  
- Check battery health

## Common Causes (in order of likelihood)

1. **Throttle twisted during power-up**
2. **Moisture or corrosion** in the throttle module
3. **Damaged throttle cable** near the handlebar hinge or stem
4. **Loose connector** between throttle and controller
5. **Failed Hall sensor inside throttle**

---

## Step-by-Step Fix (Practical)

### 1. Reset correctly
- Power scooter **off**
- Wait **30–60 seconds**
- Power **on without touching the throttle**

If the error clears → issue was startup position.

---

### 2. Inspect throttle physically
- Check throttle **returns freely** to zero
- Look for stiffness or grinding
- Ensure no bar end or grip is rubbing

---

### 3. Check wiring
- Inspect cable exiting the throttle (this is the most common failure point)
- Check for kinks or crushed insulation
- If foldable stem: check wiring inside hinge area

---

### 4. Reseat connector
- Open stem or deck (model dependent)
- Unplug and re-plug the throttle connector firmly
- Look for green corrosion or moisture

---

### 5. Test workaround (diagnostic)
If your model allows:
- Power on **with throttle disconnected**
  - If Error 16 disappears → throttle is confirmed faulty

---

**Throttle replacement**  
They are inexpensive and usually plug-and-play.

Typical specs:
- 3-wire Hall throttle
