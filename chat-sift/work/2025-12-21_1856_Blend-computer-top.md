## Best method: **Layer Mask + soft gradient**

### Photoshop
1. **Select the OK Computer PNG layer**
2. Click **Add Layer Mask** (white mask appears)
3. Select the **Gradient Tool**
   - Gradient: **Black → Transparent**
   - Type: **Linear**
   - Opacity: **100%**
4. Make sure you’re painting **on the mask (not the image)**
5. Drag **from the top downward**, only a short distance  
   - Think: *subtle fade, not full dissolve*

👉 Result: top gently blends away, bottom stays crisp.

---

## Fine-tuning (important for your piece)
After the gradient:
- Switch to a **soft round brush**
- Opacity: **10–20%**
- Paint **black on the mask** only where the mountains will overlap
- Keep the **road lines and figures intact** lower down

This keeps the OK Computer texture but lets the mountains “sit into it”.

---

## If it still looks too harsh
Try one (or both):
- **Feather the mask**:  
  `Properties panel → Feather: 5–15 px`
- Add a **very slight Gaussian Blur** to the *mask only* (2–4 px)
