[M2] AI (2026-05-23 12:30):
Neither, really. That entity["brand","Evo-Stik"] “Sticks Like” is typically an **MS polymer / hybrid polymer adhesive-sealant**.

So it sits somewhere between silicone and polyurethane:

- **Not epoxy**  
  - Epoxy is normally a 2-part resin + hardener system that cures rigid and very hard.
  - This is a 1-part cartridge adhesive.

- **Not standard silicone**  
  - Silicone stays more rubbery and is usually weaker structurally.
  - “Sticks Like” products are generally stronger grab/bond adhesives.

What you’ve got is best thought of as:
- a **flexible construction adhesive**
- with some sealing/weatherproofing capability
- good adhesion to wood, metal, masonry, plastics, etc.
- remains slightly elastic after cure

That’s why it says:
- “all weather”
- “sticks to wet surfaces”
- “resists movement”

It’s actually pretty good stuff for your sort of cabin/gate/general fabrication jobs because it tolerates movement and outdoor exposure better than brittle adhesives.

Typical behaviour:
- Initial tack/grab: moderate
- Full cure: usually ~24–48 h depending on thickness
- Finish after cure: tough rubbery solid, not hard like epoxy

For your earlier wood-to-wood ideas:
- This is much better than silicone.
- Good for vibration/movement.
- But for heavily loaded joints, screws/bolts still matter.
