Should a design get past cdr if it required mods and has t been up issued to capture these mods even though they are work links and component changes 

[M2] AI (2026-02-05 08:40):
No. A design **should not pass CDR** in that state.

### Rationale (CDR intent)
A **Critical Design Review (CDR)** formally confirms that:
- The design is **complete, stable, and producible**
- All **identified changes are captured in the released design baseline**
- The configuration under review is the **exact configuration intended for build, test, and qualification**

If modifications are required **and not captured by an up-issued design**, the design is **not configuration-controlled** and therefore **not CDR-ready**.

---

### Specific issues in your scenario

1. **Uncaptured modifications**
   - Work links, component substitutions, or routing changes are **design changes**
   - Even if they appear “minor” or “obvious,” they alter:
     - Electrical behaviour
     - Reliability
     - Manufacturability
     - Traceability

2. **Traveller ≠ design baseline**
   - A traveller can **authorise temporary deviations**
   - It **cannot replace** an updated schematic, BoM, and layout for CDR
   - CDR reviews the **design authority data**, not production workarounds

3. **Configuration control breach**
   - Passing CDR without an up-issued design creates:
     - Mismatch between *approved design* and *built hardware*
     - Audit and compliance risk
     - Ambiguity in later FA, rework, or failure analysis

---

### When *could* CDR proceed?
Only in a **very tightly defined exception**, all of the following must be true:

- Changes are formally logged as:
  - **Known deltas**
  - With a **committed up-issue** scheduled immediately post-CDR
- The CDR output explicitly states:
  - “CDR approved **subject to design issue X.Y** capturing listed changes”
- The changes are:
  - Non-architectural
  - Non-requirements-impacting
  - Non-safety-critical
- Governance accepts the **residual risk**

Even then, this is **CDR with conditions**, not a clean pass.

---

### Correct governance position (best practice)

| Status | Acceptable at CDR |
|------|------------------|
| Work links instead of schematic capture | ❌ No |
| Component changes not in BoM | ❌ No |
| Layout changes not released | ❌ No |
| Traveller-only fixes | ❌ No |
| Fully up-issued design reflecting all mods | ✅ Yes |
