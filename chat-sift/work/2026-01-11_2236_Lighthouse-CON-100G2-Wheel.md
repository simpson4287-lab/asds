That wheel is the **thermal head pressure / paper thickness adjustment dial** on the Lighthouse CON-100G2.

### What it does
It adjusts how firmly the print head is pressed against the paper:

- **Lower setting** → lighter pressure  
  Used for thin paper or label stock to avoid excessive drag or smearing.
- **Higher setting** → higher pressure  
  Used for thicker paper, labels, or slightly textured stock to ensure even heat transfer and consistent print density.

### Why it matters
Thermal printers rely on **controlled contact pressure** between the head and the paper. If the pressure is:
- **Too low** → faint or uneven printing, missed lines  
- **Too high** → premature print-head wear, paper feed issues

### How to set it (practical guidance)
1. Start at the **mid position** (often marked around “2–3”).
2. Print a short test.
3. If print is faint or uneven → increase slightly.
4. If paper drags, squeals, or head runs hot → decrease slightly.

This is a mechanical adjustment, not a calibration control, so small changes are sufficient.
