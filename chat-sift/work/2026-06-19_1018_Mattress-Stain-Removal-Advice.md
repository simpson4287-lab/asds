[M2] AI (2026-06-19 10:18):
Use the **Dr. Beckmann Upholstery Stain Remover** — the smaller bottle on the left.

A mattress is closer to **upholstery/fabric** than carpet, and that head looks gentler/smaller for working the surface without soaking it.

For a mattress:

1. **Blot first**, don’t scrub hard.
2. Use the upholstery one **lightly** — don’t saturate the mattress.
3. Work from the **outside of the stain inward**.
4. Wipe with a barely damp cloth after.
5. Let it dry fully with airflow before putting bedding back on.

The **carpet one** may work, but it’s more aimed at tougher pile fibres and larger areas. I’d only use that if the upholstery one doesn’t shift it and after a small patch test.

If it’s **urine/vomit/sweat/body odour**, an **enzyme cleaner** is usually better than either of these.

[M4] AI (2026-06-19 10:24):
That plan is broadly okay, but the main risk is **over-wetting the mattress**. A portable carpet cleaner can pull moisture back out, but mattresses hold water deep in the foam/fabric and can go musty if left damp.

I’d do it like this:

1. **Do not add much more liquid now**
   You’ve already pre-treated with water/light detergent, so let that dwell 5–10 mins, then extract it first with the carpet cleaner.

2. **Use the Dr. Beckmann Upholstery one, not the carpet one**
   Apply it lightly to the surface only. Don’t press loads of product into the mattress. Use the brush gently over the stained/odour areas.

3. **Short dwell**
   5–10 mins is enough. Don’t leave it wet for ages.

4. **Extract thoroughly**
   Use the portable cleaner mainly as a **suction tool**. Do several slow dry passes. If it has a rinse function, use only a very light rinse, then more suction passes.

5. **Drying is the important bit**
   Stand the mattress on its side if possible, window open, fan on it, dehumidifier if you have one. Don’t put sheets back on until it feels totally dry, ideally overnight.

For odour specifically, after it’s fully dry, sprinkle **bicarbonate of soda** over the mattress, leave several hours or overnight, then vacuum. If it’s a body/sweat/dog/urine-type odour, an **enzyme cleaner** will usually beat Beckmann because it breaks down the source of the smell rather than just cleaning the fabric.

Also, remove that extension lead/plug from the bed area while you’re using liquid or extraction — just not worth the risk.
