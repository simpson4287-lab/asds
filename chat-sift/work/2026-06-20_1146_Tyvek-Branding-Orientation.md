[M2] AI (2026-06-20 11:46):
Yes — **generally the Tyvek logo/branding side faces outwards, towards the weather side**.

So in your case, fitting it behind timber/cladding as a breather membrane:

**Outside/weather side:** printed Tyvek writing visible  
**Inside/warm side:** plain side facing the timber/cavity/structure

The key thing is that Tyvek breather membrane is **water-shedding but vapour-open**, so the printed side outward helps match the intended lap/installation orientation and makes inspection easier.

Also make sure:

- Upper sheets lap **over** lower sheets, like roof tiles.
- Vertical laps are generous and ideally taped.
- Don’t leave it exposed to UV/weather longer than necessary before covering.
- Any taped joints or penetrations should shed water outward, not trap it.

So yes: **branding/writing to the outside/weather side before you commit.**
