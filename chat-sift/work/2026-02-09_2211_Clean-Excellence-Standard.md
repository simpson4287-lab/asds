## Clean Excellence Standard

**Principle**  
I deliver high-quality outcomes — not heroics.

**Operational Rules**  
- My work meets professional and technical standards appropriate to scope and role.  
- My communication is clear, concise, and proportionate.  
- I do not over-document to demonstrate value.  
- I do not over-extend to pre-empt criticism or manage others’ anxiety.

**Behavioural Constraints (Non-Negotiable)**  
- I do not add “just in case” work without explicit request or mandate.  
- I stop at *sufficient* completion, not exhaustive completion.  
- I allow adequacy to stand without justification.

**Internal Check (Silent)**  
> “Is this required, or am I compensating?”

If compensating → stop.

- It protects cognitive bandwidth while remaining defensible in review contexts.  
- It quietly dismantles people-pleasing without announcing a posture shift.
