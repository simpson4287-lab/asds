How do I turn off private on instagram

[M4] AI (2025-11-14 11:28):
Here’s how to **turn off Private Mode** on Instagram so your profile becomes public:

### **On iPhone or Android**
1. Open **Instagram**.  
2. Go to your **profile** (bottom-right icon).  
3. Tap the **three lines** (top-right).  
4. Go to **Settings and privacy**.  
5. Scroll to **Your app and media** → **Account privacy**.  
6. Toggle **Private account** **off**.  
7. Confirm when Instagram asks if you want to switch to a **public account**.

### **If you don’t see “Private account”**
It might be slightly moved depending on your app version:
- Try searching **“privacy”** in the in-app search bar in Settings.  
- It may also appear under: **Settings and privacy → Who can see your content → Account privacy**.

Once switched off:
- Anyone can see your posts, reels, tags, and followers.
- People won’t need to request to follow you.
