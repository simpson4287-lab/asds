- A place for conclusions

## What goes into 5.0

### 1. School material
- Full reports (verbatim)
- Teacher comments
- Behaviour notes
- Repeated phrasing across years
- Marginal notes, effort comments, attendance remarks

### 2. Parental / contextual info (without interpretation)
- What *was* said at the time
- What *was not* supported
- Any dismissive language (quoted, not analysed)

### 3. Your own recollections
- Sensory, social, emotional experiences
- Patterns you noticed *then* (even if you didn’t have words)
- Confusion, overwhelm, masking, shutdown, avoidance

### 4. Medical / educational overlap
- ADHD traits
- Anxiety mentions
- “Bright but…”
- “Underachieves relative to ability”
- “Needs encouragement / reassurance”
- “Distracted / daydreaming / withdrawn”
