These guns only fire when the **front safety plunger** is fully depressed. If that part is sticky, dry, or slightly bent, changing the adjustment can stop it engaging properly.

If it’s an electric brad/staple gun that has sat unused, old grease can stiffen. Then it may only barely cycle in one adjustment range.

### 4. Weak drive due to drag, not lack of power
If it fires weakly even on the working setting, the striker/driver may be dragging from dirt or partial seizure.

### 5. Wrong fasteners / partial jam
Even if it “fires”, a slightly wrong strip of brads or a half-jam in the magazine/driver channel can make performance look like a power problem.
