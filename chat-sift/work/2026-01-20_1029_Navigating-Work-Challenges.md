---

### 0 – Pre-Project (PP): Stabilisation & Framing Layer

This layer exists **before** any action, disclosure, or formal positioning. Its job is not output. Its job is **orientation**.

Think of it as the control room where instruments are checked before deciding whether the mission proceeds, aborts, or simply holds course.

---

#### Purpose of 0-PP  
To convert a diffuse, emotionally loaded situation into a **bounded, intelligible system**.

Not to argue.  
Not to persuade.  
Not to defend.  

Just to establish what is *known*, what is *felt*, and what is *assumed*—and to keep those three from contaminating each other.

---

#### What Lives in 0-PP (and what must not)

**Allowed in 0-PP**
- Raw notes
- Screenshots, dates, verbatim quotes
- “This happened → I interpreted it as X → it affected me as Y”
- Unresolved contradictions
- Uncomfortable ambiguity

**Explicitly banned from 0-PP**
- Conclusions
- Judgements of intent
- Strategy
- Messaging
- “What I should say”

If something smells like rhetoric, it doesn’t belong here.

---

#### Internal Structure of 0-PP

You can think of it as three parallel tracks that never merge at this stage.

**Track A — Factual Spine**  
Chronology only.
- Date / time
- Who
- Medium (meeting, Teams, email, verbal)
- What was actually said or done  
No adjectives. No interpretation. If you can’t imagine it being read aloud in a tribunal without embarrassment, rewrite it.

**Track B — Subjective Impact (Private)**  
This is where honesty lives.
- Stress, confusion, pressure, health impact
- Moments you felt cornered, rushed, undermined, or unsure
- What you *thought* was being implied  

This track is not for sharing. Its value is self-validation and later sense-checking, not persuasion.

**Track C — Assumptions & Unknowns**  
This is the most underrated track.
- “I assumed X because Y”
- “This was never stated explicitly”
- “I don’t know whether A or B is true”
Naming uncertainty prevents it from quietly hardening into belief.

---

#### Output of 0-PP (and this matters)

The output is **not** a document to send.

The output is:
- reduced anxiety
- restored temporal order
- confidence that nothing important is being forgotten
- the ability to say, truthfully: *“I know what happened, even if I don’t yet know what it means.”*

Only when that condition is met does anything move forward.

---

#### Relationship to Later Phases

Everything that comes later—formal appraisal input, HR correspondence, legal advice, even calm verbal responses—**must be downstream of 0-PP**.

Nothing flows upward into it.
Nothing leaks sideways out of it.
It remains intact even if never used again.

That’s what makes it safe.

---

This framework gives you a psychologically sane place to stand. From there, you can choose restraint without feeling silenced, and preparation without feeling aggressive. That balance is the whole point of the project.
