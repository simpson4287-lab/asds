Here’s a **clear, step-by-step guide** to enabling **Copilot in Outlook**, plus the common reasons it doesn’t show up (very common).

---

## 1️⃣ Check you actually have Copilot entitlement (most important)
Copilot in Outlook **does not come with standard Office 365**.

You **must have one of these**:
- **Microsoft 365 Copilot add-on** (paid, per-user licence)
- A work or school account where IT has enabled Copilot

❌ **Copilot is NOT available** for:
- Microsoft 365 Personal / Family
- Most home Office installs
- Older perpetual Office versions (Office 2019/2021)

---

## 2️⃣ Confirm your Outlook version
Copilot only appears in **new Outlook builds**.

### Windows (Classic Outlook)
1. Open **Outlook**
2. Go to **File → Office Account**
3. Check:
   - **Subscription Product** (not “Office 2021”)
   - Version should be **Current Channel** or **Monthly Enterprise**

If you’re on **Semi-Annual Channel**, Copilot may not appear yet.

### New Outlook for Windows
Copilot works best here.

To switch:
- Top-right → **Try the new Outlook**

---

## 3️⃣ Enable Copilot inside Outlook (when licensed)
Once licensed and on a supported version:

### In Outlook (New or Web)
- Open an **email draft**
- Look for **Copilot ✨** or **“Draft with Copilot”**
- Or a **Copilot pane** on the right

### In Outlook on the Web
1. Go to **outlook.office.com**
2. Settings ⚙ → **Copilot**
3. Ensure it’s **enabled**

---

## 4️⃣ Make sure connected experiences are ON
Copilot will not appear if these are disabled.

1. Outlook → **File → Options**
2. **Trust Center → Trust Center Settings**
3. **Privacy**
4. Enable:
   - ✅ Connected experiences
   - ✅ Optional connected experiences

Restart Outlook after changing.

---

## 5️⃣ Sign out & back in (surprisingly important)
After licence changes:
1. Close Outlook
2. Sign out of **Office**
3. Restart PC
4. Sign back in

Copilot can take **up to 24 hours** to appear after licensing.

---

## 6️⃣ Why Copilot still isn’t showing (most common causes)

| Reason | Outcome |

Personal Microsoft account | ❌ Won’t appear |

Classic Outlook only | ⚠️ Limited support |
