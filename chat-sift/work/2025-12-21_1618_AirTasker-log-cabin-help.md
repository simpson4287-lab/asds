## Will an unassembled **entity["company","Dunster House","uk garden buildings manufacturer"]** log cabin last outside?

### ✅ Generally: **Yes**
Dunster House log cabins are supplied as:
- **Kiln-dried, untreated timber**
- **Shrink-wrapped into packs**
- Intended to tolerate **short- to medium-term outdoor storage**

Many customers buy in autumn/winter and build in spring without issues.

---

## ⚠️ The key risks (and how to avoid them)

### 1️⃣ **Ground moisture (biggest risk)**
**Never** leave the packs directly on:
- Soil
- Grass
- Paving slabs without airflow

**Do this instead:**
- Raise the packs **100–150 mm** off the ground
- Use:
  - Timber battens
  - Pallets
  - Concrete blocks + battens

👉 Airflow underneath is essential.

---

### 2️⃣ **Water trapped in the wrapping**
The factory shrink-wrap:
- Keeps rain *off*
- But can trap condensation *in*

**Best practice**
- Leave the main wrap intact
- BUT:
  - Slice **small ventilation holes** underneath or at the ends
  - Or loosen the wrap slightly once delivered

Never fully unwrap it.

---

### 3️⃣ **Sun exposure**
Winter sun is low, but:
- Direct sun on wrapped timber can cause:
  - Uneven drying
  - Slight warping

**Ideal location**
- Shaded side of the house
- North-facing wall
- Under a temporary tarp or carport (not airtight)

---

### 4️⃣ **Duration reality check**
Typical safe storage times (UK climate):

- **2–3 months** → totally fine  
- **4–5 months (autumn → spring)** → fine *if raised & ventilated*  
- **6+ months** → still possible, but inspect monthly

---

## 🧠 Extra reassurance (important)
Log cabin timber:
- Is **designed to live outdoors**
- Will be **treated after assembly anyway**
- Minor surface marks or light mould (rare) plane/sand off easily

The *only* serious failures come from:
- Ground contact
- Standing water
- Fully sealed plastic with no airflow

---

## ✅ Recommended setup (simple + safe)
If you want the gold-standard approach:

1. Pallets or battens on slabs
2. Cabin packs on top
3. Original wrap mostly intact
4. Breathable tarp loosely over the top
5. Ventilation at ends
6. Quick visual check every few weeks
