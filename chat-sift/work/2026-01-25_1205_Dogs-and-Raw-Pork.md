Can dogs have raw pork 

[M2] AI (2026-01-25 12:05):
Short answer: **No — dogs should not be fed raw pork.**

**Reasons:**
- **Parasites:** Raw pork can carry *Trichinella* and other parasites that can infect dogs.
- **Bacterial risk:** Higher likelihood of *Salmonella* and *E. coli*, which can make dogs (and humans handling it) ill.
- **Aujeszky’s disease (pseudorabies):** Rare in the UK but potentially fatal to dogs; associated with raw or undercooked pork.

**If pork is given at all:**
- It **must be fully cooked** (no pink, no raw fat).
- **No seasoning, salt, onions, garlic, or sauces.**
- Feed **plain, lean portions only**, and in moderation.

**Safer alternatives (raw or cooked, species-appropriate handling):**
- Chicken
- Turkey
- Beef
- Lamb
- Fish (boneless, appropriate species)

If this is for a **raw-feeding (BARF) diet**, pork is generally excluded unless it has been **frozen and processed to specific safety standards**, and even then many vets advise avoiding it entirely.

If you want, specify **puppy vs adult**, **raw-fed vs kibble-fed**, and **UK sourcing**, and guidance can be tightened further.
