### **If the piercing is new or still healing**
- Sleeping with a hoop is *not recommended*.
- Hoops can move and twist during the night, which can slow healing or cause irritation.
- A stud is usually recommended for the initial healing stage because it stays more stable.

### **If the piercing is fully healed**
- Yes, you **can** sleep with a hoop in.
- Many people do, but some find it uncomfortable or prone to snagging on hair or bedding.

### Tips if you want to sleep with a hoop
- Use **small, smooth, seamless hoops** – less likely to catch.
- Avoid hoops with **charms or sharp edges**.
- Silk pillowcase or hair tied back can help reduce snagging.
- If it feels sore or irritated, take a break and swap to a stud at night.
