### When to Consider Alternatives
For **extreme radiation (>100 Mrad)** or where weight and reliability are critical (space/launch vehicles), consider **optical fibre links** as an alternative interconnect. Fibre doesn’t suffer from ionising radiation in the same way as polymer-insulated copper and supports very high serial rates (multi-Gb/s) with low signal attenuation.
