## **B6 – Charges & Court Process**

**Status:** Procedural spine  
**Tone:** Factual, authoritative, non-interpretive  
**Change frequency:** Moderate (updated only as the case progresses)

### **Purpose**
To record the formal legal progression of the case from charge decision through court disposal.  
This layer represents the **official procedural timeline** and is relied upon to understand *where the case is* at any given point.

### **What This Layer Supports**
- Formal charges and charge wording
- Statutory references (as charged)
- Court venue(s)
- Hearing dates and outcomes
- Plea position(s)
- Bail status and conditions
- Sentencing outcomes or case disposal
- Adjournments, listings, and directions

### **What This Layer Does NOT Support**
- Evidence discussion  
- Interview analysis  
- Mental health context  
- Defence strategy or mitigation drafting  
- Personal reflections or expectations  

(Those belong in B2, B4, B5, or Project A as appropriate.)

---

### **Typical Contents**
- Charge sheet(s)
- Notice of charge / postal requisition
- Court summons / Single Justice Procedure notice (if applicable)
- Record of plea entered
- Case management hearing notes (factual only)
- Bail / conditional bail documentation
- Court orders and outcomes
- Sentencing notice (if reached)

---

### **Structure Within B6**
Recommended internal headings:

1. **Charge Summary**
   - Offence(s) as charged  
   - Date of charge  
   - Charging authority  

2. **Court Details**
   - Court name  
   - Jurisdiction  
   - Case reference number  

3. **Hearings**
   - Date  
   - Type of hearing  
   - Outcome / directions  

4. **Plea Record**
   - Plea entered  
   - Date entered  

5. **Bail / Conditions**
   - Bail status  
   - Conditions (if any)  
   - Review dates  

6. **Outcome / Status**
   - Pending / adjourned / concluded  
   - Sentence or disposal (when applicable)

---

### **Authoritative Rule**
If a charge, date, or outcome is **not recorded in B6**, it is **not treated as procedurally real** for Project B, regardless of expectation or discussion elsewhere.
